Top 5 AI Subscriptions of 2026: Claude, ChatGPT, Gemini and Beyond

As of June 2026, Claude Pro, ChatGPT Plus, Google AI Pro, Perplexity Pro and SuperGrok all cost around $20/month — but they're very different products. Here's which one is actually worth it for your use case.

By 2026, an AI subscription is no longer just a chatbot. For roughly $20 a month, you can get a research engine, a coding agent, a document processor, and a project workspace — sometimes all in one. The problem: they nearly all cost the same. Claude Pro, ChatGPT Plus, Google AI Pro, and Perplexity Pro all land at $20/month. So which one is actually worth your money?
