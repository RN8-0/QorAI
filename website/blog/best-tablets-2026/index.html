The 5 Best Tablets of 2026: iPad Pro M5, Galaxy Tab S11 Ultra and the Rest

Apple's June 2026 price hikes reshaped the tablet market. iPad Pro M5, iPad Air M4, Galaxy Tab S11 Ultra, OnePlus Pad 4 and Xiaomi Pad 8 Pro — which one actually makes sense now? A full comparison by price, performance and use case.

2026 has been a strange year for tablets. The hardware is better than ever — and so are the prices, in the worst possible sense. On June 25, 2026, Apple raised prices across its Mac and iPad lineups; the entry-level iPad went from $349 to $449. The iPad Air jumped $150 to $749, and the iPad Pro rose $200 to $1,199.

The cause isn't tariffs or a product refresh. It's memory. AI data centers are buying up DRAM and NAND capacity at a scale that squeezes consumer electronics makers out of the supply chain. DRAM prices rose roughly 98% in Q1 2026, and Gartner forecasts no meaningful relief until late 2027. Counterpoint research director Tarun Pathak notes memory prices have risen more than fourfold since Q4 2025, and expects other PC and tablet makers to follow Apple's lead.

That changes the question. "Best tablet" is no longer about which device is fastest — it's about whether you're actually getting your money's worth. Here are the five best tablets you can buy as of July 2026, sorted by who they're actually for.
