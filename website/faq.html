Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about Compair

Compair is an AI-powered product comparison platform that helps you make informed purchasing decisions. We analyze tech products across multiple categories including smartphones, laptops, GPUs, tablets, and more, providing detailed comparisons, compatibility scores, and personalized recommendations.
Simply paste a product URL from any supported retailer (Amazon, Best Buy, Hepsiburada, etc.) and our AI will automatically extract product details, analyze specifications, compare with similar products, and generate a personalized compatibility score based on your preferences and usage patterns.
The Compatibility Score (0-100) measures how well a product matches your specific needs. It's calculated using your browsing history, quiz responses, budget range, preferred categories, and usage patterns. The higher the score, the better the product fits your profile.
The PC Builder wizard guides you through selecting compatible components step by step — from CPU to cooler. Our AI validates compatibility at each step and suggests the best options based on your budget and previous selections. It tracks total cost and estimated power consumption throughout the build.
Your interaction data (quiz answers, browsing patterns, comparisons) is used to improve both your personal recommendation profile and our global algorithm. Personal data is anonymized before being used in global model training. You can review our Privacy Policy for full details.
We currently support 40+ categories including: Smartphones, Laptops, Tablets, Desktops, Monitors, TVs, Headphones, Speakers, Cameras, Smartwatches, GPUs, CPUs, RAM, SSDs, Motherboards, Power Supplies, PC Cases, Coolers, Dashcams, Drones, Networking equipment, and many more. New categories are added regularly.
Navigate to the Compare section and select "Subscriptions" to see side-by-side comparisons of streaming services, cloud storage, AI tools, gaming subscriptions, and more. Our AI provides analysis of value, features, and which service best fits your needs.
