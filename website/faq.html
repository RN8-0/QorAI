General 5 questions
Qor AI is an AI-powered product advisor and comparison platform available on iOS and Android. It helps you make smarter purchasing decisions by intelligently analyzing and comparing tech products — smartphones, laptops, GPUs, headphones, tablets, and 40+ other categories. Simply tell Qor AI what you're looking for, and it delivers side-by-side comparisons, personalized compatibility scores, and clear recommendations tailored to your needs and budget. Think of it as having a knowledgeable tech expert in your pocket, available 24/7.
Qor AI uses advanced AI models (Google Gemini and DeepSeek) combined with a curated product database and real-time data to analyze products across hundreds of specifications. The process is simple:
  • Search & Select — Search for any product and add it to your comparison list.
  • AI Analysis — Our AI evaluates specs, benchmark scores, value for money, and compatibility with your profile.
  • Compatibility Score — Receive a personalized 0–100 score showing how well each product fits your specific needs.
  • Smart Recommendation — Get a clear winner with transparent reasoning.
  • AI Chat — Ask follow-up questions and dive deeper through natural conversation.

AI Features 5 questions
Qor AI's AI is powered by Google Gemini and uses a curated database of verified product specifications and benchmark data. Comparisons are highly accurate for factual specifications. Recommendations are personalized based on your stated preferences, use case, and budget — they are thoughtful suggestions, not absolute certainties. We always recommend cross-checking critical purchasing decisions with official product pages and retailer listings. Please see our Terms of Service for our full disclaimer on AI-generated content.
AI Link Analysis is one of Qor AI's most powerful features. Paste a product URL from any e-commerce site — Amazon, Best Buy, Newegg, eBay, and many more — and Qor AI's AI will:
  • Automatically extract the product's specifications and details
  • Evaluate the listing price versus current market value
  • Compare it against similar products in our database
  • Calculate a personalized compatibility score just for you
  • Highlight potential concerns, advantages, and alternatives
This is especially useful when shopping online — just share the link and get an instant, unbiased analysis before you buy.
The AI chat assistant is your personal tech advisor. You can ask virtually anything about products and purchasing decisions:
  • "Is the iPhone 16 Pro worth upgrading from my iPhone 13?"
  • "What's the best laptop under $800 for video editing?"
  • "Should I buy a GPU now or wait for the next generation?"
  • "What are the key differences between these two headphones?"
  • "Is this Amazon listing a good deal right now?"
The assistant maintains full context throughout your conversation, so follow-up questions are understood naturally without needing to repeat yourself.
The Compatibility Score (0–100) is a personalized metric that measures how well a product fits your specific needs — not just how good the product is in general. It factors in:
  • Your stated use cases (gaming, creative work, photography, etc.)
  • Your budget range and value-for-money expectations
  • Your existing device ecosystem and software preferences
  • Your performance and feature priorities
A score of 85+ indicates an excellent match. Scores below 50 suggest the product may not align well with your requirements. You can update your preferences anytime in Settings → My Profile.
Qor AI uses a combination of leading AI models to deliver fast, accurate, and insightful results. Our primary AI engine is Google Gemini, which powers product comparisons, link analysis, and conversational AI features. We also integrate DeepSeek models for specific analytical tasks. Product search is powered by Algolia for lightning-fast, typo-tolerant results across our entire product catalog. Our infrastructure is continuously updated to incorporate the latest AI advancements.

Privacy & Security 4 questions
We collect only what's necessary to provide a great experience. This includes your account information (email, display name), your product preferences and settings, your comparison history to power personalized recommendations, and basic usage analytics to improve the app. We do not sell your personal data to any third parties — ever. Your data is stored securely on our PocketBase-powered servers. For complete details, please read our Privacy Policy.
Yes. Qor AI is designed with privacy as a core principle and complies with applicable data protection regulations. You have the right to access your personal data, correct inaccuracies, request deletion, and export your data in a portable format. You can exercise these rights directly from the app (Settings → Privacy) or by emailing us at contact@arain.digital. We respond to all data requests within 30 days. For full details, see our Privacy Policy.
You can permanently delete your account and all associated data in two ways:
  • In-app: Go to Settings → Account → Delete Account. This immediately and permanently removes all your data from our servers.
  • Via email: Send an account deletion request to contact@arain.digital with the subject "Account Deletion Request". We'll process it within 30 days.
⚠️ Deletion is permanent and cannot be undone. We recommend exporting your data first if you want to keep a copy (Settings → Export My Data).
Your personal data is never used to train external AI models. Anonymized, aggregated usage patterns (such as which product categories are popular) may be used to improve Qor AI's internal recommendation algorithms. Your conversations with the AI assistant are processed by our AI providers to generate responses, but are not stored for model training purposes beyond what is covered in the respective providers' data policies. You can review all data practices in our Privacy Policy.

Mobile App 4 questions
Qor AI is available on both major mobile platforms:
  • iOS: Download from the App Store — compatible with iPhone and iPad running iOS 14 or later.
  • Android: Download from Google Play Store — compatible with Android 8.0 and above.
Simply search for "Qor AI" in your app store, or visit qorai.app and tap the download button for your platform.
Qor AI requires an internet connection for most core features, since AI comparisons and real-time product data rely on our servers. However, some data is cached locally for quick access:
  • Recently viewed product comparisons are cached and viewable offline
  • Your profile preferences and settings are stored locally on your device
  • Product images are cached after first load for fast browsing
New AI comparisons, link analyses, and chat always require an active internet connection.
Yes! Premium users can export comparison results in multiple formats. Open any comparison result and tap the Share / Export button to export as a PDF summary or share a direct link. You can also export your full account data — all comparisons, preferences, and history — from Settings → Export My Data. This produces a complete JSON file of everything Qor AI has stored for your account, which you can keep for your own records.
You have full control over notifications. In the app, go to Settings → Notifications to customize what alerts you receive. Available notification types include:
  • Price drop alerts for products you're tracking
  • New product releases in your favorite categories
  • App updates and new feature announcements
  • Subscription renewal reminders
You can also manage all app notifications through your device's system settings at any time.
