Detailed analysis

The Samsung Galaxy Z Flip 5 (256 GB) stands out in the foldable smartphone market with its refined design and powerful internal hardware. Powered by the Qualcomm Snapdragon 8 Gen 2 chipset and 8 GB of RAM, it delivers exceptional performance for both everyday tasks and demanding applications, ensuring a smooth and responsive user experience. The device features a stunning 6.7-inch Dynamic AMOLED 2X main display with a 120 Hz refresh rate, offering vibrant colors and deep blacks. A key highlight is the expanded 3.4-inch Flex Window cover screen, which significantly enhances usability by allowing more interactions and widget access without unfolding the phone, a substantial improvement over its predecessor. Camera capabilities are strong, with a 12 MP main camera featuring OIS and PDAF, complemented by a 12 MP ultrawide lens. While the hardware is similar to the Z Flip 4, software optimizations driven by the new processor result in improved image quality, particularly in daylight and video stabilization. The cover screen also facilitates high-quality selfies using the main cameras. Battery life, at 3700 mAh, is generally considered adequate for a foldable device, offering over a day of moderate use. However, heavy users may find themselves needing to recharge before the day ends. Charging is supported by 25W wired and 15W wireless options, though a charger is not included in the box. In terms of software, the Z Flip 5 launched with Android 13 and One UI 5.1.1, and has received consistent updates, including Android 16 and One UI 8.5. Samsung's commitment to long-term software support ensures the device remains current with the latest features and security patches. Build quality includes an improved hinge mechanism, glass cover, and aluminum frame, along with an IPX8 water resistance rating. This provides a degree of durability, but the foldable screen remains a point of concern for some users regarding long-term wear and tear. Potential buyers should consider the delicate nature of foldable displays. Compatibility and ecosystem integration are strong within the Samsung universe, offering seamless connectivity with other Samsung devices. The phone supports 5G and Dual SIM, providing modern connectivity options. Its compact form factor when folded makes it highly portable and pocket-friendly. Overall, the Galaxy Z Flip 5 is an excellent choice for users seeking a compact, stylish, and powerful smartphone with the unique benefits of a foldable design. Its performance, enhanced cover screen, and software support are significant advantages. However, potential buyers should weigh the trade-offs regarding battery life and the reported long-term durability of the foldable screen and its protector.

Strengths

  • The Qualcomm Snapdragon 8 Gen 2 chipset delivers flagship-level performance, ensuring smooth operation for all applications and games.
  • The significantly larger 3.4-inch Flex Window cover screen greatly enhances usability, allowing for more interactions without unfolding the phone.
  • The compact, foldable design makes the device highly portable and easy to carry in small pockets or bags.
  • IPX8 water resistance provides robust protection against water immersion, adding to the device's durability for everyday use.
  • Samsung's commitment to regular software updates, including major Android versions and security patches, ensures long-term device relevance and security.
  • Improved camera performance, especially with software enhancements from the new processor, offers better photo and video quality compared to its predecessor.

Weaknesses

  • The 3700 mAh battery capacity may not be sufficient for heavy users, potentially requiring mid-day recharging.
  • The 25W wired charging speed is slower compared to many competitors in the premium segment, leading to longer charging times.
  • The charging adapter is not included in the box, requiring an additional purchase for new users.
  • The foldable screen, despite improvements, is inherently more delicate than traditional smartphone displays, raising long-term durability concerns.
  • The main camera resolution of 12 MP is lower than many competing flagship smartphones, which often feature 48 MP or 50 MP sensors.

Things that change the decision

Screen and Screen Protector Issues

Some owners have reported recurring problems with the inner screen and its protector, including peeling and touch responsiveness issues after approximately one year of use. This can lead to costly repairs if not covered under warranty, impacting the device's longevity.

Service Processes and Warranty

Reports indicate that some users faced difficulties with warranty claims or required expensive chassis replacements for screen-related issues. Understanding the manufacturer's service policy for foldable screens is crucial before purchase.

What owners say

Satisfaction: 70/100

Community sentiment for the Samsung Galaxy Z Flip 5 (256 GB) is generally positive, with a notable appreciation for its innovative design and performance. Many users are particularly impressed with the expanded Flex Window cover screen, finding it significantly more functional and convenient than previous iterations. The device's compact form factor and premium build quality also receive widespread praise, contributing to a high satisfaction rate among those who value portability. Performance is another strong point, with the Snapdragon 8 Gen 2 chipset delivering a consistently smooth and responsive experience across various applications. Owners frequently commend the phone's speed and ability to handle multitasking and demanding games without lag. The camera, while not having the highest megapixel count, is often cited for its improved image processing and overall quality, especially for casual photography and video. However, the community's positive outlook is tempered by recurring concerns regarding battery life. Many heavy users report that the 3700 mAh battery struggles to last a full day, often necessitating a mid-day recharge. This is a common point of contention and a significant factor for potential buyers to consider. Another area of concern revolves around the long-term durability of the foldable screen and its protector. While the hinge mechanism is improved, there are documented instances of screen protector peeling, display malfunctions, and even black spots appearing after several months of use. These issues, though not universal, contribute to a degree of apprehension about the device's longevity. Despite these drawbacks, the unique appeal of a foldable phone, combined with Samsung's software support and ecosystem, keeps overall satisfaction relatively high. The Z Flip 5 is seen as a stylish and capable device, but one that requires users to be mindful of its inherent foldable characteristics and battery limitations.

  • Screen Malfunctions After Android Updates occasional
    There are occasional reports of screen failures or glitches occurring specifically after major Android software updates, suggesting potential software-hardware compatibility issues that can be disruptive.
  • Hinge Problems occasional
    While improved, some users still experience occasional issues with the hinge mechanism, such as stiffness or not fully closing, which can impact the device's folding functionality over time.

Frequently asked questions

How is the performance of the Samsung Galaxy Z Flip 5?

The Samsung Galaxy Z Flip 5 is equipped with a Qualcomm Snapdragon 8 Gen 2 chipset and 8 GB of RAM. This combination delivers a fluid experience in daily use and demanding applications, ensuring top-tier performance.

Is the Galaxy Z Flip 5's battery life sufficient?

The device's 3700 mAh battery capacity and 25W fast charging are acceptable for a foldable phone, but it may struggle to last a full day with heavy use. The absence of a charging adapter in the box adds an extra cost.

What about the screen durability of the Samsung Galaxy Z Flip 5?

The improved hinge mechanism and IPX8 water resistance are positive features. However, some users have reported issues with the screen protector degrading and screen problems after about a year, raising concerns about long-term durability.

How good is the camera on the Galaxy Z Flip 5?

The 12 MP main and ultra-wide-angle cameras, thanks to software enhancements from the Snapdragon 8 Gen 2, deliver satisfactory photo and video quality. Good exposure and fast focusing, especially in daylight, are notable.

Does the Samsung Galaxy Z Flip 5 offer software support?

Yes, the device launched with Android 13 and has received regular updates up to Android 16 and One UI 8.5. Samsung's long-term software support ensures the device remains current and secure.

Is the Galaxy Z Flip 5 suitable for gaming?

Thanks to its Snapdragon 8 Gen 2 processor, the Galaxy Z Flip 5 offers powerful performance capable of running demanding games smoothly. Its 120 Hz Dynamic AMOLED 2X main screen also enhances the gaming experience.
