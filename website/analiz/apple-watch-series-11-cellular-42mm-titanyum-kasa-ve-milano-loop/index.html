Frequently asked questions

What is the battery life of the Apple Watch Series 11 Cellular 42mm?

It offers up to 24 hours of battery life with normal use, extending to 38 hours in low-power mode. The fast charging feature allows it to reach 80% charge in 30 minutes.

What health tracking features does this model offer?

It is equipped with comprehensive health features such as heart rate, SpO2, ECG, sleep tracking, fall, and crash detection. It also provides advanced features like sleep apnea and hypertension notifications.

Which phones are compatible with the Apple Watch Series 11 Cellular 42mm?

It offers full compatibility with iPhone 11 or newer models running iOS 26 or later. It is not compatible with Android phones.

Cellular connectivity (LTE, UMTS) allows you to make calls, send messages, and stream music directly from your watch, even when your iPhone is not nearby. This feature may require an additional service subscription.

The 1.77-inch always-on LTPO3 OLED display offers 2000 nits of brightness, ensuring excellent readability even in direct sunlight. The titanium case model also includes a sapphire front crystal.
