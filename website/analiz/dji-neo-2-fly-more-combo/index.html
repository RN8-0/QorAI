Detailed analysis

The DJI Neo 2 Fly More Combo Drone is positioned as an accessible yet capable drone, particularly appealing to beginners and content creators. Its design prioritizes ease of use, featuring intuitive controls like gesture and palm-based operations, alongside advanced safety measures such as omnidirectional obstacle sensing. This combination ensures a user-friendly experience, reducing the learning curve often associated with drone piloting. Performance-wise, the drone is equipped with a 12 MP 1/2-inch CMOS sensor, capable of recording 4K video at 60 frames per second and slow-motion at 4K/100fps. The inclusion of a 2-axis gimbal stabilization system, complemented by EIS, ensures that footage remains smooth and professional-looking. While the advertised flight time is 57 minutes, real-world usage typically ranges between 8-13 minutes per battery, a common discrepancy in drone specifications. The Fly More Combo addresses the flight time limitation by including three intelligent flight batteries and a two-way charging hub, significantly extending the practical operational period. This is a crucial addition for users planning longer shooting sessions or multiple flights. The drone also boasts 49 GB of internal storage, which is a convenient feature, reducing the immediate need for an external SD card. Compatibility is broad, supporting various control methods including a dedicated remote controller (RC-N3 with OcuSync 4 for up to 10 km range), mobile app control, and innovative gesture/voice controls. It also offers FPV support with optional goggles and controllers, catering to users seeking a more immersive flying experience. Its compact and lightweight design (151g) makes it highly portable and convenient for travel. Longevity is supported by DJI's reputation for software updates and ecosystem integration. The drone's robust feature set, including multiple intelligent flight modes like ActiveTrack and MasterShots, ensures it remains relevant for evolving creative needs. The integrated propeller guards also contribute to its durability, protecting against minor impacts. Potential risks include the discrepancy between advertised and real-world battery life, which the Fly More Combo largely mitigates. Some users have reported occasional gimbal errors, motor overload issues, and connectivity problems, which, while not widespread, can be frustrating. These issues often require troubleshooting steps such as recalibration or software updates. For buyers, the DJI Neo 2 Fly More Combo represents a strong investment for those seeking a portable, easy-to-use drone with excellent camera capabilities and extended flight time. Its comprehensive safety features make it an ideal starting point for new pilots, while its advanced shooting modes appeal to aspiring content creators. The Fly More Combo is highly recommended for maximizing the drone's utility.

Strengths

  • Exceptional portability due to its ultra-light 151g weight and compact design, making it easy to carry and often exempt from registration.
  • High-quality 4K/60fps video recording and 4K/100fps slow-motion capabilities with a 12 MP 1/2-inch CMOS sensor for crisp visuals.
  • Comprehensive omnidirectional obstacle sensing system enhances safety, particularly beneficial for beginner pilots.
  • The Fly More Combo includes three batteries, significantly extending the practical flight time beyond the single-battery limitation.
  • Multiple intuitive control methods, including gesture, voice, and palm control, offer a highly user-friendly experience.
  • Features 49 GB of internal storage, reducing the immediate need for an external SD card and simplifying media management.

Weaknesses

  • Real-world flight time per battery is significantly shorter (8-13 minutes) than the advertised 57 minutes, requiring frequent battery swaps.
  • Lightweight design (151g) can make the drone susceptible to instability and drift in moderate to strong winds.
  • The 2-axis gimbal, while effective, is less advanced than 3-axis systems found in higher-end models, potentially limiting stabilization in certain scenarios.
  • Lack of foldable arms, despite its compact size, means it doesn't collapse as much as some competitors for ultimate portability.
  • While it has internal storage, it lacks an optical zoom feature, limiting creative flexibility for distant subjects.

Things that change the decision

Gimbal and Motor Issues

Some users have reported initial gimbal errors and motor overload issues, including problems with the roll axis and calibration. DJI suggests checking for obstructions and recalibrating, but these can be frustrating for new users.

Connectivity and Software Stability

Occasional issues with connecting to the DJI Fly app or remote controller, as well as firmware update problems, have been reported. These typically require troubleshooting steps like restarting devices or updating software.

Wind Resistance

Due to its lightweight design (151g), some users note the drone can experience instability in windy conditions. This limits its optimal use to calmer weather, which is a common trade-off for ultra-portable drones.

Frequently asked questions

What is the flight time of the DJI Neo 2 Fly More Combo?

While the catalog states 57 minutes, real-world tests show a single battery provides around 8-13 minutes of flight time. The Fly More Combo package significantly extends this duration with three batteries.

How is the camera quality of the DJI Neo 2?

The DJI Neo 2 features a 12 MP 1/2-inch CMOS sensor camera capable of 4K/60fps video recording. Its 2-axis gimbal stabilization helps achieve professional-looking and smooth footage.

Is the DJI Neo 2 Fly More Combo safe to fly?

Yes, it offers a safe flying experience, especially for beginners, thanks to its omnidirectional obstacle sensing system (with LiDAR and Vision Positioning System) and integrated propeller guards, which help prevent collisions.

How does the DJI Neo 2 perform in windy conditions?

Due to its lightweight design (151 grams), it may experience swaying or control difficulties in windy conditions. This can affect stability during outdoor shoots and requires careful operation.

What is the internal storage of the DJI Neo 2 Fly More Combo?

The drone comes with 49 GB of internal storage. This allows users to store a significant amount of video and photos without needing an external SD card, adding to its convenience.
