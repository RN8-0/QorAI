Detailed analysis

The Samsung Galaxy S24 Ultra (512 GB) stands as a flagship smartphone, embodying Samsung's commitment to cutting-edge technology and user experience. Announced in January 2024, it has rapidly established itself in the premium segment, offering a comprehensive suite of features designed for power users and creative professionals. Its robust hardware and innovative software integration position it as a strong contender in the high-end market. At its core, the S24 Ultra is powered by the Qualcomm Snapdragon 8 Gen 3 chipset, an 8-core processor clocked at 3.39 GHz, paired with a substantial 12 GB of RAM. This configuration ensures exceptional performance across all applications, from intensive gaming to complex multitasking and advanced AI features. The 512 GB of UFS 4.0 internal storage provides ample space and rapid data access, though the absence of expandable storage is a notable design choice. The device's camera system is a major highlight, featuring a 200 MP main sensor with an F1.7 aperture, OIS, and advanced autofocus technologies. This is complemented by a 50 MP 5x optical zoom telephoto, a 10 MP 3x optical zoom telephoto, and a 12 MP ultrawide lens, offering unparalleled versatility for photography and 8K video recording. The 12 MP front camera also delivers high-quality selfies and 4K video. The 6.8-inch Dynamic AMOLED 2X display is a visual marvel, boasting a QHD+ resolution (1440x3120 pixels), a 120 Hz adaptive refresh rate, and an astounding 2600 nits peak brightness. Protected by Corning Gorilla Glass Armor, the flat screen design minimizes reflections and enhances durability. This display provides an immersive and vibrant viewing experience, even in bright outdoor conditions. Powering this sophisticated hardware is a 5000 mAh Lithium-Ion battery, supporting 45W fast charging, wireless charging, and reverse wireless charging. While the battery life is generally excellent, some users have noted that the charging speed, while fast, might not be the absolute quickest compared to certain rivals. The device also features IP68 water and dust resistance and a premium titanium frame, underscoring its durable and high-quality construction. Samsung's commitment to software longevity is a significant advantage, with the S24 Ultra promising seven years of major Android OS updates and security patches. This extended support ensures the device remains relevant and secure for a prolonged period, maximizing its return on investment. The integrated S Pen further enhances productivity, offering precise input for various tasks, from note-taking to photo editing. However, its top-tier specifications, robust build, and extensive software support justify this price for users seeking a device that excels in performance, camera capabilities, and long-term usability. It is a device built to last and perform at the highest level for years to come.

Strengths

  • The Qualcomm Snapdragon 8 Gen 3 processor provides industry-leading performance, ensuring smooth operation for all applications and demanding games.
  • The versatile 200 MP main camera system, alongside advanced telephoto lenses, delivers exceptional photo and 8K video quality across various lighting conditions.
  • The 6.8-inch QHD+ Dynamic AMOLED 2X display offers stunning visuals with a 120 Hz refresh rate and an impressive 2600 nits peak brightness for superior outdoor visibility.
  • Samsung's commitment to seven years of major OS updates and security patches guarantees extended software longevity and continued feature enhancements.
  • The robust build quality, featuring a titanium frame and IP68 water/dust resistance, ensures high durability and protection against daily wear and tear.
  • The integrated S Pen provides unique productivity features, enhancing precision for tasks like note-taking, drawing, and document annotation.

Weaknesses

  • The retail package is minimalist, excluding a power adapter, which necessitates an additional purchase for new users.
  • Despite supporting 45W fast charging, some users report that the full charging time can be longer than expected for a flagship device.
  • The absence of a microSD card slot means storage is not expandable beyond the internal 512 GB, which could be a limitation for heavy users.
  • The premium price point places it at the higher end of the smartphone market, making it a significant investment.
  • While the S Pen is a powerful tool, users who do not utilize stylus functionality may find this feature less relevant to their needs.

Things that change the decision

Minimalist Box Contents

The retail box includes only a SIM eject pin and a USB Type-C to Type-C cable, requiring users to purchase a separate power adapter. This adds an unforeseen cost for those without a compatible charger, impacting the immediate out-of-box experience.

Software Update Risks

Despite a long update commitment, some users have reported issues with camera performance and battery drain following specific software updates. This indicates potential instability with new firmware, which could temporarily degrade the user experience.

Absence of Storage Expansion

The device does not support microSD card expansion, meaning users are limited to the internal 512 GB storage. While substantial, heavy users of media or large applications might eventually face storage constraints without cloud solutions.

What owners say

Satisfaction: 87/100

Community sentiment for the Samsung Galaxy S24 Ultra (512 GB) is overwhelmingly positive, reflecting its status as a top-tier device. Users frequently laud its exceptional camera system, highlighting its ability to capture stunning photos and videos across various conditions, including challenging low-light environments. The display quality also receives widespread acclaim, with its brightness, QHD+ resolution, and vibrant colors providing an immersive visual experience that stands out. Performance is another area where the S24 Ultra consistently earns high marks. The powerful Snapdragon 8 Gen 3 processor ensures a fluid and responsive user experience, handling demanding applications and games with ease. Many users appreciate the device's speed and efficiency, making it a reliable daily driver for power users. However, the community sentiment isn't entirely without nuance. While the 5000 mAh battery generally provides good all-day endurance, some owners have observed a relatively quick drop from 100% to 90% charge, which, while not a critical flaw, is a recurring observation. The 45W fast charging, though capable, is sometimes perceived as slower than what competitors offer, leading to minor dissatisfaction among those accustomed to ultra-fast charging technologies. A more significant concern revolves around software updates. Despite Samsung's commendable commitment to seven years of updates, some users have reported experiencing issues with camera functionality and increased battery drain following specific firmware releases, particularly after the July 2026 update. These instances suggest that while long-term support is a strength, the quality control of individual updates can sometimes be inconsistent, temporarily impacting user satisfaction. These reports, while not universal, indicate a potential area for improvement in software stability post-update. Overall, the Samsung Galaxy S24 Ultra is highly regarded for its premium features and robust capabilities. The positive feedback on its core strengths, such as camera, display, and performance, far outweighs the minor criticisms regarding battery initial drain and update-related glitches. The integrated S Pen is also a beloved feature for those who utilize its productivity enhancements, further solidifying its appeal among a specific user base.
