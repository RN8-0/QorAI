Detailed analysis

This hardware, coupled with 12 GB of RAM and 512 GB of internal storage, provides a robust platform for intensive applications, high-fidelity gaming, and significant media creation. The device's announcement in January 2024 confirms its contemporary status in the smartphone market. Performance and quality are paramount in the S24 Ultra. Samsung's commitment to software updates, typically extending over several years for flagship devices, further contributes to its long-term viability. The high initial investment also represents a financial risk, making careful consideration of its features against personal needs essential. Strengths

  • The Qualcomm Snapdragon 8 Gen 3 processor delivers industry-leading performance, ensuring smooth operation for heavy gaming, video editing, and demanding multitasking.
  • The 6.8-inch QHD+ Dynamic AMOLED 2X display offers stunning visuals with 1440x3120 resolution, HDR10+, and an exceptional 2600 nits peak brightness for superior outdoor visibility.
  • Integrated S Pen provides precise input for detailed drawing, note-taking, and photo editing, enhancing productivity and creative workflows.
  • The versatile 200 MP main camera system, alongside advanced telephoto lenses, captures highly detailed photos and videos across various focal lengths, including impressive zoom capabilities.
  • A robust 5000 mAh battery provides excellent all-day power, even under heavy usage, reducing the need for frequent recharges.
  • Constructed with a titanium frame and Corning Gorilla Armor, the device offers superior durability and scratch resistance, protecting against daily wear and tear.

Weaknesses

  • The 45W fast charging, while capable, is not the fastest available on the market, which might be a drawback for users accustomed to ultra-rapid charging speeds.
  • The device's premium price point makes it a significant investment, potentially placing it out of reach for budget-conscious consumers.
  • The inclusion of a USB Type-C port for audio output means users with traditional 3.5mm headphones will require an adapter, which is not always convenient.
  • The physical size and weight of the device, typical for an Ultra model, might be cumbersome for users preferring a more compact and lighter smartphone.
  • The anti-reflective properties of the screen may be diminished if a third-party screen protector is applied, and Samsung does not offer an official compatible protector.

Things that change the decision

Battery Longevity vs. User Expectation

While the 5000 mAh battery is large, achieving two full days of heavy use, especially with constant gaming and video editing, may be challenging. Users should manage expectations for multi-day endurance under peak loads.

S Pen Integration and Ecosystem

The S Pen is integrated, making it a powerful tool for creative work, but its full potential is realized within Samsung's ecosystem. Users transitioning from other brands might need time to adapt to the software features.

Locked Bootloader for Power Users

For users who desire deep customization, root access, or alternative operating systems, Samsung's locked bootloader presents a significant limitation. This restricts advanced user control over the device's software.

What owners say

Satisfaction: 88/100

Community sentiment for the Samsung Galaxy S24 Ultra (512 GB) is overwhelmingly positive, with a strong consensus that it is a leading Android flagship. Owners frequently highlight the device's stunning display, powered by Corning Gorilla Armor, as a major selling point, noting its brightness and clarity even in challenging lighting conditions. The performance, driven by the Snapdragon 8 Gen 3, is consistently praised for its speed and ability to handle intensive tasks like gaming and video editing without noticeable slowdowns, satisfying power users. The camera system also garners significant acclaim, particularly for its versatility, zoom capabilities, and overall image quality. Many users appreciate the detailed shots and excellent video stabilization. The integrated S Pen is a beloved feature among those who utilize it for productivity and creative endeavors, underscoring its value as a unique differentiator in the smartphone market. However, the community is not without its criticisms. A notable point of contention revolves around battery life, where some users report inconsistent drain, especially following certain software updates. While many find the battery sufficient for a full day, the expectation of two full days of heavy use often goes unmet, leading to some disappointment among those with high demands. Display-related issues also surface, with a segment of users reporting problems with the anti-reflective coating wearing off or experiencing minor visual glitches like diagonal lines on dark backgrounds. These issues, while not universal, indicate potential quality control variations or specific vulnerabilities. Additionally, some users express frustration over Samsung's locked bootloader, limiting advanced customization options for a device otherwise built for power users. Overall, the S24 Ultra is seen as a highly capable and desirable device, particularly for its core strengths in display, performance, and camera. The criticisms, while valid, tend to be specific pain points for certain user groups rather than widespread fundamental flaws, suggesting that the device largely lives up to its premium billing for the majority of its owners.

What owners keep praising

  • Dynamic AMOLED 2X Display
    Owners consistently rave about the screen's vibrant colors, exceptional brightness, QHD+ sharpness, and the effectiveness of the anti-reflective Corning Gorilla Armor, making it a joy for all visual tasks.
  • Snapdragon 8 Gen 3 Performance
    The raw power and efficiency of the Snapdragon 8 Gen 3 chipset are frequently praised, allowing for seamless multitasking, demanding gaming, and smooth video editing without any lag or performance drops.
  • Versatile Camera System with Zoom
    The camera is a standout, with users highlighting its ability to capture stunning detail, impressive optical zoom capabilities (especially the 5x telephoto), and excellent video stabilization, making it a go-to for diverse photography.
  • Premium Build Quality
    The titanium frame and Gorilla Armor contribute to a sense of robustness and durability, giving owners confidence in the device's ability to withstand daily use and minor impacts.

Chronic problems

  • Thermal Throttling and Overheating common
    The S24 Ultra can experience severe thermal throttling during heavy gaming or demanding emulation, leading to significant frame rate drops and the phone becoming uncomfortably hot even during minimal use like with wired Android Auto.
  • Battery Drain and Charging Issues common
    Users have reported severe battery drain and overheating after the One UI 7 and July security updates, with rapid battery loss during active use and idle periods, and some experiencing fast charging failures.
  • Display Graininess ('Mura') and Vividness Issues widespread
    Many S24 Ultra owners have reported a grainy or denim-like texture on the display, particularly at low brightness on dark backgrounds, and an initial issue where the 'Vivid' display mode appeared muted, though an update was released to address the latter.
  • Green/White Line Display Defects occasional
    Some S24 Ultra units have exhibited a thin, bright green vertical line, and sometimes a horizontal white line, on the display, typically indicating a hardware failure that appeared shortly after launch or after firmware updates.
  • Camera Inconsistencies and Defects common
    Numerous camera issues have been reported, including inconsistent colors, soft/grainy/blurry photos, overexposure, inaccurate white balance, and abnormal red colors, particularly in low light and with high-resolution modes, with some improvements noted after firmware updates.
