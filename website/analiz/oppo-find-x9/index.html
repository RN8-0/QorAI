Detailed analysis

The Oppo Find X9, launched in October 2025, positions itself as a premium smartphone with a strong emphasis on display quality, camera performance, and battery longevity. It features the MediaTek Dimensity 9500 chipset, offering significant performance gains and improved power efficiency compared to its predecessors. The device runs on Android 16 with ColorOS 16, promising a refined user experience and extensive software support. Performance-wise, the Dimensity 9500 ensures a smooth and responsive experience for daily tasks, gaming, and multitasking. However, under sustained heavy loads, such as intense benchmarking or prolonged gaming sessions, the device may exhibit performance throttling. This behavior is common in high-performance smartphones but is a point of consideration for users who consistently push their devices to the limit. The camera system is a highlight, boasting a triple 50 MP setup co-engineered with Hasselblad. The main camera, utilizing a LYT-808 sensor, excels in capturing detailed and color-accurate images, particularly in low-light conditions. While the overall camera quality is impressive, the absence of autofocus on the front camera is a minor drawback for selfie enthusiasts. Its 6.59-inch 120Hz AMOLED display is a visual treat, offering vibrant colors, deep blacks, and exceptional brightness, peaking at 1800 nits. The display also supports Dolby Vision and HDR, providing an immersive viewing experience for multimedia content. The slim bezels and durable Corning Gorilla Glass 7i protection further enhance its premium feel. Battery life is another strong suit, with a massive 7025 mAh capacity that can comfortably last up to two days with normal usage. The inclusion of 80W wired SUPERVOOC and 50W AIRVOOC wireless charging ensures rapid power replenishment, minimizing downtime. This combination makes the Find X9 an excellent choice for users who prioritize battery endurance. In terms of durability, the Oppo Find X9 features an aluminum frame and boasts IPX9 water resistance and IP6X dust durability, indicating a high level of protection against environmental elements. Oppo's commitment to six years of software updates for ColorOS 16 ensures the device remains current and secure for an extended period, adding to its long-term value. Connectivity options are comprehensive, including dual SIM support, 5G capabilities, and Bluetooth 6.0 with a wide range of audio codecs. However, potential buyers should be aware that NFC support may vary by region. The device's compatibility with Oppo's magnetic power bank and 50W AIRVOOC Magnetic Charger also enhances its ecosystem appeal.

Strengths

  • Exceptional 120Hz AMOLED display with 1800 nits peak brightness and Dolby Vision support for immersive visuals.
  • Massive 7025 mAh battery providing up to two days of usage, coupled with 80W wired and 50W wireless fast charging.
  • Hasselblad-tuned triple 50 MP camera system delivering natural, detailed, and consistent photos, even in low light.
  • Powerful MediaTek Dimensity 9500 chipset offering smooth performance for daily tasks and gaming.
  • Robust build quality with an aluminum frame and IPX9 water resistance and IP6X dust durability.
  • Long-term software support with a promise of six years of Android and ColorOS updates.

Weaknesses

  • Performance throttling under sustained heavy loads can reduce peak performance during intense use.
  • The front-facing camera lacks autofocus, potentially affecting the sharpness of selfies and video calls.
  • UFS 4.1 storage write speeds have been noted as weaker in some tests, possibly impacting large file transfers.
  • NFC support may be region-dependent, which could limit contactless payment options for some users.
  • Audio output is exclusively via USB Type-C, requiring an adapter for traditional 3.5mm headphones.

What owners say
