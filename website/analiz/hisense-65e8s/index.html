Detailed analysis

The Hisense 65E8S Ultra HD (4K) TV, released in 2026, positions itself as a strong contender in the upper-mid segment with its advanced display and gaming features. It leverages Mini LED technology, combined with Quantum Dot Display and Direct LED backlighting, to deliver a vibrant and high-contrast 4K picture on its 65-inch screen. The inclusion of the Hi-View AI Engine further refines image quality by optimizing color, contrast, and sharpness in real-time, alongside effective 4K upscaling. Performance-wise, the TV excels in visual fidelity and motion handling. Its native 144Hz refresh rate, complemented by 288Hz Smooth Motion technology, ensures exceptionally fluid on-screen movement, which is particularly beneficial for fast-paced content and gaming. The wide viewing angles provided by the IPS-ADS panel technology mean consistent image quality from various seating positions, making it suitable for larger family rooms. For HDR content, the 65E8S offers comprehensive support, including Dolby Vision, HDR10+, and HLG, along with specialized modes like Filmmaker Mode and IMAX Enhanced certification. This broad compatibility ensures that viewers experience content as intended by creators, with dynamic range and accurate colors. The reported peak brightness of 1600 nits contributes to striking HDR highlights. The audio experience is also a highlight, featuring a 2.1 channel, 40W RMS sound system tuned by Devialet, which includes a built-in subwoofer. This setup provides powerful and clear sound, often negating the immediate need for an external soundbar. Dolby Atmos and Dolby Audio support further enhance the immersive soundstage. Gaming performance is a significant strength, with two HDMI 2.1 ports supporting 4K at 144Hz, VRR, ALLM, and AMD FreeSync Premium. These features combine to deliver a low-latency, tear-free, and highly responsive gaming experience, making it an excellent choice for console and PC gamers. The dedicated Game Bar provides quick access to essential gaming settings. The smart TV functionality is powered by Hisense's VIDAA operating system. While VIDAA provides access to popular streaming services and integrates with smart home ecosystems like Google Home and Apple AirPlay, some users might find its interface and app selection less extensive compared to Android TV or webOS. Connectivity options are robust, including multiple HDMI ports, USB, optical audio out, and modern wireless standards like Wi-Fi 5 and Bluetooth 5.4. Overall, the Hisense 65E8S offers a compelling package for its segment. It delivers a high-quality visual experience, strong gaming capabilities, and impressive built-in audio. While the VIDAA OS might be a point of consideration for some, its core performance attributes make it a valuable investment for those prioritizing picture quality and gaming prowess.

Strengths

  • Quantum Mini LED display delivers superior contrast, deep blacks, and exceptional brightness for stunning visuals.
  • Native 144Hz refresh rate with 288Hz Smooth Motion ensures incredibly fluid and blur-free motion, ideal for sports and action.
  • Comprehensive HDR support (Dolby Vision, HDR10+, HLG) guarantees optimal dynamic range and color accuracy across all content.
  • Devialet-tuned 2.1 channel audio system with a built-in subwoofer provides powerful and immersive sound quality.
  • Advanced gaming features like HDMI 2.1, VRR, ALLM, and FreeSync Premium offer a highly responsive and tear-free gaming experience.
  • Hi-View AI Engine intelligently optimizes picture quality in real-time, enhancing color, contrast, and 4K upscaling.

Weaknesses

  • The VIDAA smart TV operating system, while functional, may offer a less diverse app selection compared to Android TV or webOS.
  • Reported typical brightness levels vary, suggesting potential inconsistencies in sustained brightness performance.
  • Only two of the four HDMI ports are HDMI 2.1, which might limit simultaneous connections for multiple next-gen devices.
  • The TV's slim design, while aesthetically pleasing, might limit internal speaker volume or bass compared to bulkier models.
  • Wi-Fi 5 (802.11ac) is included, but the absence of Wi-Fi 6 might be a drawback for users with newer network infrastructure.

Things that change the decision

VIDAA Operating System

The VIDAA OS is functional but may lack the extensive app ecosystem or customization options found in Android TV or webOS. Users accustomed to other platforms might find it less intuitive.

HDMI/eARC Connection Issues

Some owners have reported occasional handshake problems with HDMI ARC/eARC connections, particularly with soundbars. This can necessitate troubleshooting or using alternative audio outputs.
