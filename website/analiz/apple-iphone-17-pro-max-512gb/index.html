Strengths

  • The Apple A19 Pro chipset with 12 GB RAM delivers industry-leading performance, ensuring seamless operation for all applications and multitasking.
  • The 48 MP triple rear camera system offers professional-grade photography and videography, including advanced features like 4x optical zoom and improved low-light performance.
  • The 6.9-inch OLED display with 2868x1320 resolution and 120 Hz refresh rate provides stunning visuals, vibrant colors, and smooth scrolling.
  • A 5088 mAh battery capacity, coupled with 40W fast charging and wireless charging, ensures extended usage and convenient power replenishment.
  • Robust build quality with an aluminum frame and Ceramic Shield 2 front cover provides enhanced durability and scratch resistance.
  • Comprehensive 5G connectivity and seamless integration within the Apple ecosystem offer a highly connected and user-friendly experience.

Weaknesses

  • The premium price point makes it a significant investment, potentially out of reach for budget-conscious buyers.
  • Some users have reported issues with surface scratching and color degradation on specific finishes, particularly the Cosmic Orange model.
  • While powerful, the large 6.9-inch form factor may be cumbersome for users preferring a more compact device for one-handed use.
  • The device's reliance on the Apple ecosystem can be a drawback for users preferring more open-source flexibility or cross-platform compatibility.
  • Despite fast charging, the included box content only features a USB Type-C to Type-C cable, requiring users to purchase a compatible power adapter separately for optimal charging speeds.

Things that change the decision

Post-Software Update Issues

Certain iOS updates have led to complaints about lagging performance and stuck animations, particularly with iOS 26. While these issues are often resolved with subsequent patches, they can temporarily detract from the premium user experience.

Camera Software Instability

Some users have experienced camera software glitches, such as altered brightness settings after shooting or blurry images. These inconsistencies can be frustrating for users relying on the device's advanced camera capabilities for high-quality content creation.

Face ID Security Concerns

A user complaint regarding a Face ID security issue has been reported, persisting for approximately two years. While isolated, this raises a minor concern about the long-term reliability of this key biometric security feature.

While many of these issues are often resolved with subsequent patches, they can temporarily impact the user experience and create frustration. Despite these concerns, the overwhelming consensus points to a highly capable and satisfying device. The raw performance, driven by the Apple A19 Pro chip, is consistently highlighted as a major strength, ensuring a fluid and responsive experience even under heavy load.
