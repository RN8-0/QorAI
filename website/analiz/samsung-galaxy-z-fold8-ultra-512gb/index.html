Detailed analysis

The Samsung Galaxy Z Fold8 Ultra (12 GB / 512 GB) is a cutting-edge foldable smartphone designed for users who prioritize expansive screen real estate and top-tier performance. It features a powerful Qualcomm Snapdragon 8 Elite Gen 5 processor with 8 cores, clocked up to 4.74 GHz, paired with a generous 12 GB of RAM. This configuration ensures seamless multitasking, demanding application handling, and a fluid user experience, positioning it as a performance leader in the smartphone market. The device boasts an impressive 8.0-inch foldable Dynamic LTPO AMOLED 2X main display and a 6.5-inch cover display, both offering vibrant visuals and a smooth 120Hz refresh rate.

Performance and quality are outstanding, driven by the advanced chipset and ample RAM. The 200 MP main camera is a standout feature, delivering exceptional image quality and rapid capture, which aligns perfectly with users who consider their smartphone their primary camera. The 5000 mAh battery provides substantial endurance, complemented by 45W fast charging and 20W wireless charging, ensuring long usage times and convenient power replenishment. The build quality, with Corning Gorilla Glass Ceramic 3 and IPX8 water resistance, suggests good durability, a crucial aspect for a premium foldable device.

Compatibility is broad, supporting 5G, Dual SIM (Nano-SIM + eSIM), Bluetooth 6.0, and USB Type-C. It runs on Android 17 with Samsung's One UI 9, offering a feature-rich and customizable software experience. Longevity is expected to be excellent due to its powerful hardware and Samsung's commitment to software updates, ensuring it remains relevant for several years. The 512 GB internal storage is more than sufficient for most users, reducing the need for frequent file management.

However, potential risks include its high price point, which makes it a significant investment. The foldable form factor, while innovative, means it is inherently thicker and heavier than traditional smartphones, potentially impacting portability and ergonomic comfort for users who prefer a compact device. While the main camera is stellar, some reviews suggest the telephoto lens might not always meet the highest expectations. The advanced Galaxy AI features, while powerful, might be underutilized by users who don't push their devices to their limits.

Buying advice for this model is nuanced. It is an excellent choice for power users, tech enthusiasts, and professionals who can leverage the large foldable display for productivity and immersive media consumption. Those who prioritize camera quality and long battery life will also find it highly appealing. However, users seeking a truly compact, pocket-friendly device or those with more basic usage needs might find its size and premium cost to be excessive. Consider your daily usage patterns and budget carefully before investing in this high-end foldable.

What owners say

Satisfaction: 78/100

Community sentiment for the Samsung Galaxy Z Fold8 Ultra (12 GB / 512 GB) is largely positive, with significant enthusiasm for its technological advancements. Users and reviewers frequently laud the device's stunning 8.0-inch foldable display, highlighting its almost flat inner screen and effective anti-reflective coating as major improvements for an immersive viewing experience. The raw power delivered by the Qualcomm Snapdragon 8 Elite Gen 5 processor is a consistent positive, with many noting its seamless performance even under heavy loads and for demanding applications. Battery life is another strong point, often cited as impressive and capable of sustaining extended usage.

However, the device's premium price point is a major recurring negative theme across various platforms, including retailer reviews and tech forums. Many consider it to be prohibitively expensive, making it a luxury item rather than a mainstream option. The physical bulkiness of the foldable design, while expected, is also a point of concern for some users who prefer more compact and pocket-friendly devices, impacting ergonomic comfort during long sessions.

There's a mixed sentiment regarding the camera system; while the 200 MP main camera receives widespread praise for its quality and speed, the telephoto camera sometimes falls short of expectations compared to other flagship devices. Some discussions also revolve around the utility of the new Galaxy AI features, with reviewers noting they are currently 'niche' and might not be fully utilized by all users, raising questions about their immediate value proposition at this price point. Competition from other foldables, including the potentially lighter Galaxy Z Fold8, is also a topic of discussion.

Overall, the community recognizes the Z Fold8 Ultra as a powerful and innovative device, pushing the boundaries of smartphone technology. The satisfaction stems from its core performance, display, and battery, but the high cost and inherent compromises of a foldable form factor, particularly regarding portability, temper the enthusiasm for a broader audience. What are the key performance features of the Samsung Galaxy S26 Ultra?

How good is the camera on the Samsung Galaxy S26 Ultra?

The S26 Ultra features a high-resolution 200 MP main camera. This allows for capturing incredibly detailed photos, offering significant flexibility for cropping and editing without losing image quality.

Is the Samsung Galaxy S26 Ultra water resistant?
