Detailed analysis

The Samsung Galaxy Z Fold8 Ultra (12 GB / 512 GB) represents the pinnacle of Samsung's foldable smartphone technology, offering a unique blend of a traditional smartphone and a tablet-like experience. Powered by the latest Qualcomm Snapdragon 8 Elite Gen 5 chipset and 12 GB of RAM, it delivers exceptional performance for multitasking, gaming, and demanding applications. Its dual Dynamic AMOLED 2X displays provide vibrant visuals, with the main folding screen offering an expansive canvas for productivity and content consumption. The device also boasts a powerful 200 MP main camera system, ensuring high-quality photography. Performance and quality are outstanding, with the 3 nm CPU manufacturing technology indicating high efficiency and raw power. The large 5000 mAh battery, coupled with 45W fast charging and wireless charging, aims to provide extended usage throughout the day. The IPX8 water resistance adds a layer of durability, protecting against accidental spills and submersion. However, the inherent nature of a foldable display means the inner screen is more susceptible to scratches than a traditional glass panel, and the visible crease can become more pronounced over time. Compatibility is broad, supporting 5G, Wi-Fi 6E, and Bluetooth 6.0, ensuring seamless connectivity with modern networks and accessories. The device runs on Android 17, offering access to a vast ecosystem of applications and services. However, the unique form factor might require some app optimization for the folding display, though Samsung generally provides robust software support for its foldables. The USB Type-C port serves for both charging and audio output. Longevity is a mixed bag. While the premium build materials like the aluminum frame and glass back suggest durability, the folding screen's vulnerability to scratches and the reduced battery cycle count (1,200 cycles compared to 2,000 in previous flagships) are notable concerns. Samsung's commitment to software updates typically extends for several years, ensuring the device remains current with the latest Android features and security patches. However, the mechanical hinge and inner display are potential long-term points of failure. Potential risks include the aforementioned screen durability issues and the possibility of thermal throttling under heavy load, which could impact sustained performance. Early software bugs have also been reported, though these are often addressed with updates. The premium price point also means higher repair costs if the specialized folding screen or hinge requires servicing. Users should consider investing in a protective case to mitigate some of these physical risks. For buyers, this phone is ideal for those who prioritize a large, flexible display for productivity and media consumption, and who appreciate cutting-edge technology. It's a powerful device for users who can leverage the dual-screen functionality for multitasking. However, those who demand a perfectly smooth, highly scratch-resistant screen and a slim, traditional form factor might find its inherent design trade-offs challenging. The device offers a unique experience but requires a conscious acceptance of its specific characteristics. Considering the user's strong preference for a perfectly flat screen and sleek profile, the Z Fold8 Ultra presents a significant compromise. While it offers unparalleled screen real estate and performance, the visible crease and thicker design directly contradict these core requirements. The user's willingness to accept some dust vulnerability is a minor positive, but the fundamental display characteristic remains a mismatch. The demand for two full days of battery life without performance impact is also ambitious for this form factor, especially with intensive use of the larger screen. The 'consider' decision reflects this balance of cutting-edge innovation against specific user preferences that are not fully met by a foldable design.

Strengths

  • Expansive 8.0-inch folding Dynamic AMOLED 2X display provides a tablet-like experience for enhanced productivity and media consumption.
  • Powered by the Qualcomm Snapdragon 8 Elite Gen 5 chipset and 12 GB RAM, offering industry-leading performance for demanding applications and multitasking.
  • Features a high-resolution 200 MP main camera system, capable of capturing incredibly detailed photos and videos.
  • Robust 5000 mAh battery capacity supports extended usage, complemented by 45W fast charging and wireless charging capabilities.
  • IPX8 water resistance ensures the device can withstand submersion, providing peace of mind against water damage.
  • Generous 512 GB internal storage offers ample space for a vast library of apps, high-resolution media, and important documents.

Weaknesses

  • The inherent folding display mechanism results in a visible crease that can become more noticeable over time, conflicting with a desire for a perfectly smooth screen.
  • The device is significantly thicker and heavier than traditional smartphones when folded, impacting pocketability and one-handed usability.
  • The inner folding screen is highly susceptible to scratches and damage, even from minor contact, requiring careful handling.
  • The silicon-carbon battery is rated for 1,200 charging cycles, a reduction from previous flagships, potentially leading to faster battery degradation.
  • The premium price point of approximately 4147 USD makes it one of the most expensive smartphones on the market, with potentially higher repair costs.
  • The cover screen, while wider than previous models, can still feel awkward for one-handed typing for some users.

Things that change the decision

Visible Display Crease

Despite initial appearances, the folding display's crease can become more noticeable over time, directly contradicting the user's need for an 'absolutely smooth screen'. This is a fundamental characteristic of current foldable technology.

Inner Screen Durability

The inner folding screen is highly susceptible to scratches and damage, even from fingernails, which is a significant concern for long-term use and contradicts expectations for a robust device. This impacts the perceived longevity of the primary display.

Thermal Throttling Concerns

Reports of the device getting uncomfortably warm during routine tasks and experiencing thermal throttling suggest potential performance limitations under sustained load. This could impact the user's desire for consistent performance.

Suboptimal Audio Experience

When unfolded in landscape mode, both speakers fire from the left side, leading to poor stereo separation and effectively mono audio. This diminishes the immersive experience for media consumption on the large screen.

What owners say

Satisfaction: 78/100

Community sentiment for the Samsung Galaxy Z Fold8 Ultra is generally positive, driven by its groundbreaking technology and enhanced productivity features. Owners frequently highlight the large, expansive inner display as a game-changer for multitasking, content consumption, and even light work, transforming their mobile experience. The device's raw performance, powered by the Snapdragon 8 Elite Gen 5, also receives strong praise, with users reporting a consistently fast and fluid experience across applications. However, the enthusiasm is tempered by recurring concerns related to the inherent characteristics of foldable phones. The most prominent issue is the visibility and feel of the display crease, which many users report becoming more pronounced with continued use. This often leads to comparisons with rival foldables that have less noticeable creases, creating a divide among users regarding the premium feel of the screen. Durability of the inner folding screen is another significant point of contention. While the outer screen is robust, the inner display's susceptibility to scratches, even from fingernails, is a frequent complaint. This raises concerns about the phone's long-term resilience and the need for extra care, which some users find inconvenient for a daily driver. Battery life draws mixed reactions. While the 5000 mAh capacity is substantial, heavy users of the large inner screen often find it challenging to achieve multi-day power, leading to a need for daily charging. Additionally, reports of the device getting warm and experiencing thermal throttling during routine tasks suggest that Samsung's thin design might be impacting sustained performance and user comfort. Other notable discussions include the suboptimal stereo separation from the speakers when unfolded, and the placement of the inner selfie camera, which some find intrusive. Early software bugs, such as lock screen layout issues and specific gesture malfunctions, were also reported by some users, although these are often addressable via software updates. The community is largely split between those who embrace the innovative form factor despite its flaws and those who find the compromises too significant for the premium price.

Chronic problems

  • Display Crease Becoming More Noticeable common
    Users report that the crease on the main folding display becomes more prominent and visible over time, detracting from the visual experience. This is an inherent characteristic of the current folding screen technology.
  • Thermal Throttling and Overheating common
    Some users experience the device getting uncomfortably warm during routine tasks, leading to thermal throttling and reduced performance. Samsung has acknowledged this and plans a software update.
  • Occasional Screen Unresponsiveness occasional
    A few users have reported the bottom portion of the screen becoming unresponsive occasionally, requiring a reboot to resolve the issue, suggesting a software or display driver glitch.

  • Xiaomi 17 Ultra (1 TB) — This alternative offers a significantly larger 6000 mAh battery and much faster 90W charging, addressing battery longevity concerns. It also provides more RAM and storage, but it is a traditional smartphone, not a foldable, and has a 50 MP main camera.

Who is it for?

Buy it if: This phone is best for power users and early adopters who prioritize an expansive, flexible display for multitasking, content creation, and media consumption, and are willing to invest in cutting-edge foldable technology despite its unique form factor trade-offs.

Skip it if: This device is not for users who demand an absolutely smooth, crease-free screen, a sleek and traditional smartphone profile, or those who prioritize maximum long-term physical durability without compromise.

Verdict

The Samsung Galaxy Z Fold8 Ultra is a technological marvel offering unparalleled screen real estate and top-tier performance. However, its inherent foldable design, including the visible crease and increased thickness, directly conflicts with preferences for a perfectly smooth and sleek device. While innovative, buyers prioritizing traditional smartphone characteristics and uncompromised screen durability should consider alternatives.

Frequently asked questions

What is the battery life of the Samsung Galaxy Z Fold8 Ultra?

The device offers up to two days of usage thanks to its 5000 mAh battery capacity and silicon-carbon technology, meeting expectations for long battery life.

How is the performance of the Z Fold8 Ultra?

The Qualcomm Snapdragon 8 Elite Gen 5 chipset provides more than sufficient stable and powerful performance for basic applications and light gaming. However, thermal throttling may occur under heavy loads.

Is the Samsung Galaxy Z Fold8 Ultra dust and water resistant?

The device has IPX8 water resistance, providing high protection against water. However, its IP48 dust resistance might be insufficient for users frequently in dusty environments.

What is the screen quality of the Z Fold8 Ultra?

Both the outer and inner screens offer a balanced and high-quality visual experience with 3000 nits peak brightness and an anti-reflective coating. Outdoor visibility is excellent.

Is the camera on the Galaxy Z Fold8 Ultra good?

With a 200 MP main camera and an upgraded 50 MP ultra-wide-angle camera, it can capture good photos, which is sufficient for general use. Telephoto performance might be weaker compared to rivals.

Is the Samsung Galaxy Z Fold8 Ultra worth its price?

While offering high performance, its high price tag and some features not fully aligning with user needs affect the price-to-performance balance. Alternatives should be considered for budget-conscious buyers.
