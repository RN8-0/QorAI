Detailed analysis

The Samsung Galaxy Z Fold8 Ultra (12 GB / 512 GB) is a premium foldable smartphone designed for users who prioritize an expansive display and high-end performance. Its 8.0-inch Dynamic AMOLED 2X main screen offers a tablet-like experience, ideal for media consumption and productivity. The device features a powerful Qualcomm Snapdragon 8 Elite Gen 5 processor, ensuring smooth operation across all tasks, from light browsing to demanding applications. With 12 GB of RAM and 512 GB of non-expandable storage, it provides ample resources for multitasking and storing extensive digital content. The camera system, led by a 200 MP main sensor, promises excellent photo quality for capturing memories, aligning with user needs for good photography without requiring every cutting-edge feature. Its 5000 mAh battery supports 45W fast charging and 20W wireless charging, balancing convenience with battery longevity. The device runs on Android 17 with One UI 9, offering a refined and optimized user experience for its unique form factor. While its foldable nature means a thicker profile and a visible crease, these are accepted trade-offs for the larger screen. The Z Fold8 Ultra is a significant investment, but it delivers a top-tier mobile experience for those who can leverage its advanced capabilities.

Strengths

  • Expansive 8.0-inch Foldable Dynamic AMOLED 2X Main Display: Offers a large, immersive screen for superior multitasking, media consumption, and productivity.
  • Cutting-Edge Qualcomm Snapdragon 8 Elite Gen 5 Processor: Delivers exceptional performance for demanding applications, gaming, and seamless multitasking.
  • High-Resolution 200 MP Main Camera: Captures incredibly detailed photos, bringing professional-grade photography capabilities to everyday moments.
  • Substantial 5000 mAh Battery with 45W Fast Charging: Provides extended usage times and rapid recharging, balancing convenience with battery health.
  • Generous 12 GB RAM and 512 GB Internal Storage: Ensures smooth operation with multiple apps and ample space for a vast digital library.
  • IPX8 Water Resistance and Corning Gorilla Glass Ceramic 3: Offers robust durability against water immersion and enhanced protection for the cover display.

Weaknesses

  • High Initial Purchase Price: The device is positioned at a premium price point, making it a significant financial investment.
  • Thicker and Heavier Form Factor: Despite improvements, its foldable design results in a thicker profile compared to traditional slab smartphones, impacting pocketability.
  • Visible Crease on Main Display: The folding screen inherently has a visible crease, which, while improved, can still be noticeable under certain conditions.
  • Non-Expandable Storage: The 512 GB storage is fixed, with no option for microSD card expansion for users needing more space.
  • S Pen Sold Separately: The S Pen, a key productivity accessory, is not included in the box and requires an additional purchase.

Things that change the decision

Foldable Form Factor Trade-offs

Despite improvements, the foldable design inherently means a thicker device when folded and a visible crease on the main display. Users must be comfortable with these physical characteristics, which are a direct consequence of the expansive screen.

Non-expandable Storage

While 512 GB of internal storage is generous, the device lacks a microSD card slot for expandable storage. Users with extremely large media libraries or those who prefer physical expansion should factor this limitation into their decision.

What owners say

Satisfaction: 87/100

Community sentiment for the Samsung Galaxy Z Fold8 Ultra is largely positive, with owners frequently expressing high satisfaction with its core features. The expansive 8.0-inch inner display is almost universally lauded, with users highlighting its utility for multitasking, media consumption, and general productivity. Many consider it a game-changer for mobile computing, transforming how they interact with their device. The powerful Qualcomm Snapdragon 8 Elite Gen 5 processor also receives significant praise, ensuring a fluid and responsive user experience even under heavy loads. This performance is a key differentiator, especially for those upgrading from older models. Furthermore, the 200 MP main camera is often cited as a major improvement, delivering impressive photo quality that satisfies most users' needs for capturing memories. The improved display technology, including a less noticeable crease and anti-reflective coating, contributes to the overall positive reception, indicating Samsung's continuous refinement of the foldable experience. However, the high price point is a consistent point of contention, with many acknowledging it as a significant barrier to entry. While the device's advancements are appreciated, some users find the Galaxy AI features to be more niche than universally essential, leading to a neutral stance on their overall impact. The telephoto camera also garners some criticism for not meeting the high expectations set by the main sensor. It is important to note that some reported issues, such as speaker placement and cover screen width, are specifically attributed to the standard Z Fold 8 model and not the Z Fold8 Ultra, which appears to have addressed these concerns.
