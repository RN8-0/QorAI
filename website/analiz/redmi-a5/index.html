The Redmi A5 presents a compelling package for budget-conscious users, excelling in battery longevity and providing a smooth visual experience with its 120 Hz display. Detailed analysis

The Redmi A5, announced in March 2025, positions itself as an entry-level smartphone designed for users who prioritize essential functionality and battery life. It features a large 6.88-inch IPS LCD display with a 120 Hz refresh rate, offering a fluid visual experience for its class. Powering the device is a Unisoc T7250 chipset with an 8-core CPU clocked at 1.8 GHz, paired with 4 GB of RAM. Performance-wise, the Redmi A5 is well-suited for daily tasks such as web browsing, social media, and video streaming. Its 12nm manufacturing process for the CPU indicates a focus on efficiency, contributing to its impressive battery life. However, the device is not intended for demanding applications or graphically intensive games, where users may encounter noticeable slowdowns. The camera setup includes a 32 MP main sensor and an 8 MP front camera, both with f/2.0 aperture. These cameras support features like portrait mode, HDR, and AI scene detection, making them capable for casual photography and video calls. Video recording is capped at 1080p 30fps for both cameras, which is standard for this segment but lacks advanced stabilization. Battery life is a significant strength, thanks to its 5200 mAh Li-ion Polymer battery, which can last up to two days under normal usage. The 15W fast charging via USB Type-C is a convenient addition, though a full charge takes approximately 137 minutes. This extended endurance makes it ideal for users who are often away from charging points. Connectivity options include Dual SIM support, Wi-Fi 5, and Bluetooth 5.2. A 3.5 mm headphone jack and FM radio are also present, catering to traditional audio needs. However, the absence of 5G connectivity and NFC are notable omissions that might deter users looking for more advanced features or future-proofing.

Strengths

  • Exceptional 5200 mAh battery capacity provides up to two days of usage on a single charge.
  • Large 6.88-inch IPS LCD display with a smooth 120 Hz refresh rate enhances visual fluidity.
  • IP54 rating offers protection against dust and splashes, improving durability for daily use.
  • Dual SIM support allows for flexible management of personal and professional contacts.
  • Dedicated 3.5 mm headphone jack and FM radio cater to traditional audio preferences.

Weaknesses

  • Lacks NFC, preventing contactless payments and quick pairing functionalities.
  • Absence of 5G connectivity limits mobile data speeds to 4G networks.
  • HD+ display resolution results in lower pixel density compared to competitors, impacting sharpness.
  • Unisoc T7250 chipset provides only basic performance, unsuitable for intensive gaming or heavy multitasking.
  • 15W fast charging is relatively slow, taking over two hours for a full battery charge.

Things that change the decision

Lack of NFC

The Redmi A5 does not include NFC, which means it cannot be used for contactless payments or quick pairing with accessories. This is a significant drawback for users accustomed to modern convenience features.

No 5G Connectivity

This smartphone lacks 5G support, limiting it to 4G networks. While 4G is sufficient for many, users in areas with robust 5G infrastructure or those planning to keep their phone for several years might find this a significant limitation.

Insufficient for Intensive Gaming

The Unisoc T7250 chipset and Mali-G57 GPU are adequate for basic tasks and casual games, but they struggle with graphically demanding titles. Serious mobile gamers should look for devices with more powerful processors.

Verdict

The Redmi A5 is a strong contender in the entry-level smartphone market, offering excellent battery life and a large, fluid display for its price point. Frequently asked questions

What is the battery life of Redmi A5?

The Redmi A5 features a 5200 mAh battery, offering up to two days of battery life with normal usage. This is one of the device's strongest aspects, reducing the need for frequent charging.

Is the Redmi A5 suitable for gaming?

The Unisoc T7250 chipset and 4 GB RAM are sufficient for basic tasks like social media and video streaming. However, its performance is limited for graphics-intensive games and heavy multitasking, making it unsuitable for serious gamers.

What are the display specifications of the Redmi A5?

The device features a 6.88-inch HD+ IPS LCD screen with a 120 Hz refresh rate and 600 nits HBM brightness, providing a fluid and adequately bright visual experience. It also has TÜV Rheinland certification for eye comfort.

Does the Redmi A5 support NFC or 5G?

No, the Redmi A5 does not support NFC, which means features like mobile payments are unavailable. Additionally, the device does not offer 5G connectivity.

How is the camera performance of the Redmi A5?

The 32 MP main camera and 8 MP front camera offer average quality photos with features like portrait mode and HDR. It delivers acceptable results in good lighting conditions but its performance is limited in low light or for detailed shots.

Will the Redmi A5 receive software updates?

Yes, the Redmi A5 ships with Android 15 (Go Edition), and Xiaomi guarantees 2 years of operating system updates and 4 years of security updates for this model.
