Detailed analysis

The Vivo X300 Ultra, introduced in March 2026 and launched in July 2026, positions itself as a premium flagship smartphone. It boasts a powerful Qualcomm Snapdragon 8 Elite Gen 5 chipset, manufactured with a 3nm process, ensuring top-tier performance for all applications, from demanding games to intensive multitasking. Performance is a significant strength, with the device demonstrating exceptional thermal management thanks to a dedicated VS1+ Pro Imaging Chip and a large vapor chamber cooling system. This allows for sustained high performance without throttling, making it a reliable choice for power users and mobile gamers. The camera system is a standout feature, co-engineered with ZEISS and featuring T* coating. It includes a 200 MP main sensor, a 50 MP ultra-wide, and a 200 MP periscope telephoto lens with 3.7x optical zoom. This configuration delivers outstanding photographic capabilities across various scenarios, particularly excelling in low-light conditions and offering versatile zoom options. The 6.82-inch QHD+ AMOLED display is another highlight, offering a sharp 1440x3168 resolution and a fluid 144 Hz refresh rate. With Dolby Vision, HDR, and HDR10+ support, the screen provides vibrant colors and deep contrasts, making it excellent for media consumption and an immersive user experience. Battery life is robust, powered by a 6600 mAh BlueVolt semi-solid-state battery that provides extended usage times, often exceeding 7 hours of screen-on time. The inclusion of 100W wired FlashCharge and 40W wireless charging ensures that the device can be quickly topped up, minimizing downtime. Software support is commendable, with Vivo committing to up to 7 years of security updates for OriginOS 6. While the OriginOS experience has been noted for some localization issues and pre-installed apps, the commitment to long-term updates is a positive for device longevity. The phone also features a premium build with a glass back, aluminum frame, and IP68/IP69 water and dust resistance, enhancing its durability. Connectivity is comprehensive, supporting 5G, Wi-Fi 7, and Bluetooth 5.4, ensuring fast and reliable network access. Its ecosystem features, including file transfer and synchronization support with Apple devices and seamless AirPods connectivity, make it a versatile option for users across different platforms. Despite its strengths, potential buyers should be aware of the software's current state, particularly regarding localization and notification management. The high price point, typical for a flagship, also means that optional accessories like the ZEISS lens kit add to the overall investment. However, for users prioritizing cutting-edge hardware, exceptional camera performance, and robust build quality, the Vivo X300 Ultra remains a highly competitive option.

Strengths

  • Features a Qualcomm Snapdragon 8 Elite Gen 5 processor, delivering industry-leading performance for all demanding tasks and gaming.
  • Equipped with a ZEISS-engineered triple camera system, including a 200 MP main sensor and a 200 MP periscope telephoto lens, offering exceptional photographic versatility and quality.
  • Boasts a stunning 6.82-inch QHD+ AMOLED display with a 144 Hz refresh rate, providing incredibly smooth visuals and vibrant colors with HDR support.
  • Integrates a large 6600 mAh semi-solid-state battery, ensuring extended usage times and reliable power throughout the day.
  • Supports ultra-fast 100W wired FlashCharge and 40W wireless charging, allowing for rapid power replenishment.
  • Constructed with a premium glass back and aluminum frame, featuring IP68 and IP69 water and dust resistance for enhanced durability.

Weaknesses

  • OriginOS 6 exhibits some localization issues and a less polished feel in non-Chinese markets, impacting the software experience.
  • The device comes with numerous pre-installed applications and persistent notifications from the V-Appstore that cannot be easily disabled.
  • Some reports indicate that the automatic brightness adjustment tends to set the screen too dark in various environments.
  • The advanced ZEISS telephoto extender lens kit, which enhances camera capabilities, is an additional purchase, increasing the total cost.
  • Despite a large battery, some tests show higher power consumption during Wi-Fi usage at standard brightness, potentially affecting efficiency.

What owners say

Satisfaction: 85/100

Community sentiment for the Vivo X300 Ultra is largely positive, with a strong emphasis on its flagship-level hardware. Users are particularly impressed by the camera system, often singling out the ZEISS optics and versatile zoom capabilities as a major draw. The phone's performance, driven by the Snapdragon 8 Elite Gen 5, also garners widespread acclaim for its speed and ability to handle demanding tasks without issue. The large battery and rapid charging speeds are consistently praised, providing users with confidence in the device's endurance and quick power recovery. The high-refresh-rate QHD+ AMOLED display is another point of satisfaction, delivering a visually stunning and smooth user experience that enhances daily interactions and media consumption. However, the software experience with OriginOS 6 is where opinions diverge. While some users find it acceptable, a notable segment expresses frustration over localization issues, particularly with untranslated text and inconsistent notification management. The presence of pre-installed apps and persistent notifications from the V-Appstore is also a recurring complaint, suggesting a desire for a cleaner, more customizable interface. Despite these software-related criticisms, the overall perception remains favorable due to the phone's robust hardware and exceptional performance in key areas like photography and processing power. The build quality and design are also well-received, contributing to the device's premium appeal. The consensus indicates that while the software may require some adaptation or tolerance, the core strengths of the Vivo X300 Ultra make it a highly desirable device for its target audience.

Chronic problems

  • OriginOS Localization and Notification Issues common
    Many users report that OriginOS 6 suffers from incomplete English translations and inconsistent notification behavior, leading to a less refined user experience, particularly outside of China. This often manifests as untranslated menu items or delayed/improperly grouped notifications.
  • Automatic Brightness Adjustment occasional
    Some owners find that the automatic brightness feature frequently sets the screen too dark in various lighting conditions, requiring manual adjustment. This can be an annoyance in daily use.
  • Software Update Glitches occasional
    A subset of users has reported experiencing minor glitches or performance hiccups after receiving software updates. While often resolved with subsequent patches, it indicates occasional instability in the update process.
