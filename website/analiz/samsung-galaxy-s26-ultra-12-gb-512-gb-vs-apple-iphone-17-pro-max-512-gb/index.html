Frequently asked questions

Is the Samsung Galaxy S26 Ultra's camera performance better than the iPhone 17 Pro Max?

The Samsung Galaxy S26 Ultra captures incredibly detailed photos with its 200 MP main camera, while the iPhone 17 Pro Max's 48 MP Fusion cameras offer superior performance, especially in video quality. Both devices have strong camera capabilities in their respective areas.

Which has a longer battery life: Samsung Galaxy S26 Ultra or iPhone 17 Pro Max?

In tests, the Apple iPhone 17 Pro Max (4832 mAh battery) performed better in terms of battery life than the Samsung Galaxy S26 Ultra (5000 mAh battery). The Galaxy S26 Ultra's battery life may fall short of expectations during heavy use.

How do the fast charging features compare between the Samsung Galaxy S26 Ultra and iPhone 17 Pro Max?

The Samsung Galaxy S26 Ultra charges quickly with 60W fast charging support, while the Apple iPhone 17 Pro Max offers 50% charge in 20 minutes with 40W fast charging. Samsung provides a higher charging speed in this aspect.

Which phone offers longer software update support?

The Apple iPhone 17 Pro Max will receive updates for at least 5-6 years due to Apple's long-term software update policy. While the Samsung Galaxy S26 Ultra also offers long-term support, Apple generally commits to a longer period.

How do the two phones compare in terms of screen quality and reported issues?

The Samsung Galaxy S26 Ultra's 6.9-inch LED display has reported calibration issues such as reddish tints, dot-like marks, and inconsistent brightness. The iPhone 17 Pro Max's 6.9-inch LED display offers a more consistent visual experience with vibrant colors and high brightness.
