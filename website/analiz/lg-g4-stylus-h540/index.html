Detailed analysis

The LG G4 Stylus (H540) is a smartphone released in April 2015, positioning it as a highly outdated device in 2026. It features a 5.7-inch LED display with a 720p resolution, powered by a MediaTek MT6592M chipset with an 8-core 1.4 GHz CPU and a meager 1 GB of RAM. Storage is limited to 8 GB, though it supports microSD expansion. Key features include a 13 MP main camera with laser autofocus, a 5 MP front camera, a 3000 mAh removable battery, and a 3.5 mm audio jack. It shipped with Android 5.0 Lollipop, with some units upgradable to Android 6.0 Marshmallow. The device also includes a basic stylus for navigation and jotting, and an IR blaster for remote control functionality. It supports Dual SIM and 3G connectivity, but lacks 4G and 5G capabilities, fast charging, or a fingerprint reader. Its plastic body and Corning Gorilla Glass 3 display offer basic durability for its time. Overall, its technical specifications are significantly below current smartphone standards, impacting performance and app compatibility. The device is generally discontinued and not available new, making it a challenging and risky acquisition. Its primary appeal lies in its original low cost and the inclusion of a stylus, which was a differentiator for its segment.

Strengths

  • The 5.7-inch LED display provides a larger viewing area, suitable for media consumption, and was considered good for its class upon release.
  • Includes a 3.5 mm audio jack, essential for users with existing wired headphones and audio equipment.
  • Features a removable 3000 mAh Lithium Ion battery, allowing for easy replacement and potentially extending the device's lifespan.
  • The presence of a stylus offers basic note-taking and navigation capabilities, a differentiating feature for its original market segment.
  • Equipped with an IR blaster, enabling the phone to function as a universal remote control for TVs and other appliances.
  • Supports Dual SIM functionality, which is convenient for managing personal and work numbers or for international travel.

Weaknesses

  • The 1 GB RAM and 1.4 GHz CPU deliver severely slow and lag-prone performance, making it unsuitable for modern applications and multitasking.
  • Only 8 GB of internal storage is highly insufficient for current app sizes and media, necessitating constant storage management or microSD expansion.
  • The stylus offers only basic functionality and lacks the precision required for detailed drawing or intricate handwriting tasks.
  • The display resolution of 720p on a 5.7-inch screen results in noticeable pixelation and a less sharp visual experience compared to modern devices.
  • Lacks modern features such as 4G/5G connectivity, fast charging, wireless charging, and a fingerprint reader, which are standard in contemporary smartphones.

Things that change the decision

Severe Bootloop Hardware Defect

The LG G4 Stylus is notorious for a widespread bootloop issue caused by a hardware defect, rendering the device inoperable. This critical flaw makes the phone highly unreliable and a significant risk to any buyer.

Outdated Android Version

The device runs on an old Android version (up to 6.0 Marshmallow), severely limiting app compatibility and security updates. Many modern applications will not function correctly or at all, making it impractical for daily use in 2026.

Limited Internal Storage

With only 8 GB of internal storage, the device struggles to accommodate even a few modern applications and media. While expandable via microSD, the base storage is a constant bottleneck and requires careful management.

What owners say

Satisfaction: 25/100

Community sentiment for the LG G4 Stylus (H540) is overwhelmingly negative, primarily driven by severe hardware reliability issues and its outdated specifications. The most critical and frequently discussed problem is the infamous 'bootloop' defect, which renders the device unusable due to a fundamental hardware flaw. This issue was widely acknowledged by LG and even led to class-action lawsuits, indicating its pervasive nature and significant impact on owner satisfaction. Many users who initially enjoyed the phone reported it failing unexpectedly after a period of use, often after overheating. This widespread failure overshadows any positive aspects the device might have had. Beyond the bootloop, owners consistently criticized the phone's sluggish performance, even at its release. The combination of a slow processor and limited RAM resulted in noticeable lag when opening apps, multitasking, or browsing the web. The low internal storage of 8GB was another major point of contention, with users struggling to install even a few essential applications and frequently needing to offload media to an SD card. The 720p display, while large, was also often cited for its visible pixelation, especially as higher-resolution screens became common. On the positive side, when the device was functional, some owners appreciated the large screen size for media consumption and the inclusion of a stylus for basic note-taking, though its precision was often compared unfavorably to more premium options. The removable 3000 mAh battery was also a loved feature, offering decent battery life for its time and the convenience of easy replacement. The presence of a 3.5mm headphone jack and the IR blaster were also frequently mentioned as valuable inclusions. However, these positive points are largely overshadowed by the device's fundamental reliability issues and its inability to keep pace with modern smartphone demands.

Chronic problems

  • Bootloop Hardware Defect widespread
    The LG G4 series, including the Stylus, is infamous for a widespread hardware defect causing the phone to enter an unrecoverable bootloop. This issue often appears after the phone heats up and is caused by faulty soldering on the main board, rendering the device inoperable.
  • Laggy Performance and Slowdowns common
    Owners consistently report the device experiencing significant lag and slowdowns, especially when opening apps, switching between them, or performing even moderately demanding tasks. This is a persistent issue stemming from underpowered hardware.
  • Touchscreen Unresponsiveness occasional
    Some users experienced issues with the touchscreen, including taps not registering correctly or delayed responses, particularly with the default LG keyboard, impacting overall usability.

Frequently asked questions

Is the LG G4 Stylus (H540) suitable for gaming?

No, the LG G4 Stylus (H540)'s 1 GB RAM and old MediaTek MT6592M processor cause slowdowns even with social media and light games. Do not expect a fluid gaming experience.

What is the battery life of the LG G4 Stylus (H540)?

The device's 3000 mAh battery and replaceable battery feature can provide all-day use with manual settings. However, the age of the device and lack of software optimization may negatively impact battery life.

How good is the LG G4 Stylus (H540) camera?

The 13 MP main camera offers basic and clear photos for capturing moments. Although supported by features like laser autofocus, it does not meet professional expectations and may be insufficient in low light.

What is the software support like for the LG G4 Stylus (H540)?

The LG G4 Stylus (H540) remains on Android 5.1 Lollipop. This is a major disadvantage for modern app compatibility and security updates, preventing many new applications from running.

Is the LG G4 Stylus (H540) storage sufficient?

No, the mere 8 GB of internal storage will fill up quickly after a few apps and photos. Continuous cloud backup or the use of an external memory card becomes mandatory.

Does the LG G4 Stylus (H540) have chronic issues?

Yes, the LG G4 Stylus (H540) series is known for chronic issues such as boot loop problems and unresponsive touchscreens. These pose significant risks to the device's longevity and reliability.
