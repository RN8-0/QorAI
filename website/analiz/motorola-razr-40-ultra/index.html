Detailed analysis

The Motorola Razr 40 Ultra, launched in June 2023, stands out as a premium foldable smartphone designed for users who value both style and functionality. It features a powerful Qualcomm Snapdragon 8+ Gen 1 chipset, ensuring smooth performance across various applications and tasks. The device's dual-display setup, with a large internal foldable screen and a highly functional external display, is a core differentiator. Performance is a strong suit, with the Snapdragon 8+ Gen 1 processor and up to 12 GB of LPDDR5 RAM providing ample power for demanding apps and multitasking. Users can expect a responsive and fluid experience, whether browsing, gaming, or switching between applications. The 512 GB UFS 3.1 storage ensures fast data access and sufficient space for digital content. The camera system, featuring a 12 MP main sensor with OIS and a 13 MP ultra-wide lens, delivers good image quality. A significant advantage is the ability to use these main cameras for high-quality selfies or video calls, leveraging the external display as a viewfinder. This enhances the device's versatility for content creation. The display technology is a highlight, with a 6.9-inch 165 Hz LTPO AMOLED main screen and an impressive 3.6-inch 144 Hz pOLED external display. The external screen's size and functionality allow users to manage notifications, quick settings, and even run full applications without unfolding the phone, greatly improving convenience. Battery life, however, is a point of contention. The 3800 mAh capacity, while supported by 30W wired and 5W wireless charging, has led to mixed user feedback. Some find it sufficient for a day, while others report faster depletion, suggesting it might not meet the demands of heavy users without midday recharging. Motorola generally offers three major Android OS updates, providing reasonable software longevity for the Razr 40 Ultra. The device launched with Android 13 and has received subsequent security patches and optimizations, ensuring a current and secure software experience. In terms of build, the phone features an aluminum frame, Gorilla Glass Victus rear panel, and a robust stainless steel hinge. Its IP52 splash resistance rating offers basic protection against water, and long-term reports indicate the hinge maintains its integrity well, addressing common concerns about foldable durability. Connectivity is comprehensive, including 5G, Wi-Fi 6E, Bluetooth 5.3, and NFC, ensuring fast and reliable wireless performance. The USB Type-C 2.0 port handles charging and data, but the absence of a 3.5mm headphone jack and external storage expansion are notable omissions that might affect some users.

Strengths

  • Large and highly functional 3.6-inch external pOLED display allows for extensive use without unfolding the phone.
  • Powerful Qualcomm Snapdragon 8+ Gen 1 chipset delivers smooth and responsive performance for all tasks.
  • High refresh rate 165 Hz LTPO AMOLED main display provides an incredibly fluid visual experience.
  • Compact, pocket-friendly foldable design offers superior portability compared to traditional smartphones.
  • Robust build quality with an aluminum frame, Gorilla Glass Victus, and a durable stainless steel hinge.
  • Versatile camera system that can be used for high-quality selfies via the external display.

Weaknesses

  • The 3800 mAh battery capacity may not provide all-day power for heavy users, requiring frequent recharges.
  • Lacks a 3.5mm headphone jack, necessitating adapters or wireless headphones for audio output.
  • No expandable storage via microSD card, limiting long-term storage flexibility beyond the internal 512 GB.
  • The phone's glossy finish can be slippery, making a protective case almost essential for secure handling.
  • Wireless charging is limited to 5W, which is slower than many competitors and less convenient for quick top-ups.

Things that change the decision

Lack of External Storage and Headphone Jack

The absence of a microSD card slot means storage is fixed, and the lack of a 3.5mm headphone jack requires USB-C adapters or wireless audio solutions. This limits flexibility for media consumption and data management.

eSIM Limitations

Some users have noted limitations or issues with eSIM functionality, which could impact connectivity options for international travelers or those relying on multiple network profiles. It's a minor but present concern for specific use cases.

What owners say

Satisfaction: 78/100

Community sentiment for the Motorola Razr 40 Ultra is generally positive, with a notable enthusiasm for its innovative design and the utility of its large external display. Users frequently highlight how the compact, foldable form factor enhances portability, making it convenient for everyday use. The external screen is a particular point of praise, often cited as a game-changer for quick interactions, notifications, and even running full applications without needing to open the phone. This feature significantly contributes to the overall positive user experience and distinguishes the device from its competitors. However, the battery life emerges as the most significant area of concern. While some users find the 3800 mAh battery adequate, a considerable portion reports faster-than-expected drain, especially under heavy usage. This suggests that while the phone is highly functional, its endurance might not satisfy all users, particularly those accustomed to larger battery capacities. The camera performance receives mixed but generally positive reviews, with many appreciating its capabilities for casual photography and vlogging, especially when leveraging the external screen for selfies. The software experience is largely seen as clean and efficient, benefiting from Motorola's near-stock Android approach, though some minor UI annoyances are occasionally mentioned. Durability, a common concern for foldable phones, is largely viewed positively for the Razr 40 Ultra. Owners frequently commend the robust hinge design and the perceived resilience of the foldable display, with reports indicating it holds up well over time.

What owners keep praising

  • Compact and Pocket-Friendly Design
    The foldable nature of the phone is highly appreciated for its ability to become very small and easily fit into pockets, enhancing portability.
  • Robust Hinge and Screen Durability
    Many users highlight the sturdy build of the hinge and the perceived durability of the foldable screen, even after a year of use, which instills confidence.

Chronic problems

  • Battery Consumption common
    Some users report that the phone's battery drains faster than anticipated, which can be influenced by background apps or software updates. This often requires more frequent charging than expected.
  • Keyboard Switching Issues occasional
    One user specifically noted that switching between different keyboard apps like Gboard and SwiftKey can be 'super annoying,' indicating a minor software glitch or inconvenience.
  • External Screen App Ordering Deficiency occasional
    The absence of an alphabetical sorting feature for applications on the external display has been raised as an inconvenience by some users, making app navigation less intuitive.
  • Slippery Body common
    The phone's smooth and glossy finish makes it prone to slipping, leading many owners to recommend or require the use of a protective case for better grip and safety.

Verdict

Frequently asked questions

How is the battery life of the Motorola Razr 40 Ultra?

The 3800 mAh battery capacity, while sufficient for average use, may struggle to last a full day with intensive use. Some users have reported rapid battery drain, which is one of the device's weaknesses.

How functional is the Razr 40 Ultra's external screen?

The 3.6-inch 144 Hz pOLED external screen is one of the device's strongest features. It offers the convenience of checking notifications, using widgets, and even running full applications when the phone is closed.

What is the camera performance like?

The 12 MP main camera and 13 MP ultra-wide sensor can capture quality photos in good lighting conditions. The 32 MP front camera and the ability to use the external screen for selfies provide added flexibility; 4K video performance for vlogging is also appreciated.

Is the Motorola Razr 40 Ultra suitable for gaming?

The Qualcomm Snapdragon 8+ Gen 1 chipset and 12 GB of RAM are sufficient to run the latest games and demanding applications smoothly. The 165 Hz refresh rate of the main screen also provides a fluid gaming experience.

What about the phone's durability and build quality?

The aluminum frame, Gorilla Glass Victus back panel, and stainless steel hinge give the device a premium feel and robustness. The IP52 splash resistance rating also provides additional protection for daily use.
