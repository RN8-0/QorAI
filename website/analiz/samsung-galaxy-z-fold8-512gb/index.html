Detailed analysis

The Samsung Galaxy Z Fold8 (12 GB / 512 GB) represents a significant evolution in foldable smartphone technology, building upon its predecessors with substantial upgrades. Powered by the cutting-edge Qualcomm Snapdragon 8 Elite Gen 5 processor and 12 GB of RAM, it delivers uncompromising performance suitable for the most demanding applications and intensive multitasking scenarios. This hardware foundation ensures a fluid user experience across its innovative dual-screen setup. Its display technology is a standout feature, boasting a 5.5-inch cover screen and a 7.6-inch main foldable display, both utilizing Dynamic AMOLED 2X panels with a 120 Hz adaptive refresh rate. The peak brightness of 3000 nits ensures excellent visibility even under direct sunlight, while the refined hinge mechanism results in a significantly less noticeable crease. This enhances both the aesthetic appeal and the immersive quality of the large internal display. Photography capabilities are solid, featuring a 50 MP main camera and a 50 MP ultra-wide-angle lens, capable of 8K video recording. While it lacks a dedicated telephoto lens found in some competitors, the primary cameras offer strong performance for everyday photography and creative shots. The ability to use the main cameras for high-quality selfies by unfolding the device adds versatility. Battery life is improved with a 4800 mAh capacity, providing reliable endurance for a full day of use, even with its power-hungry displays. The 45W fast wired charging and 20W wireless charging support ensure quick top-ups, minimizing downtime. This enhanced battery performance addresses a common concern with earlier foldable models. Durability is addressed through an Armor Aluminum frame, Gorilla Glass protection, and an IPX8 water resistance rating, making it robust against daily wear and tear. The device's reduced thickness and weight mark it as the lightest Z Fold to date, improving its portability and handling. This makes it more comfortable to use as a daily driver, whether folded or unfolded. Samsung's commitment to seven years of software updates, starting with Android 17 and One UI 9, is a major advantage. This extended support ensures the device receives regular security patches and feature enhancements, prolonging its lifespan and keeping it competitive for years to come. This makes the Z Fold8 a future-proof investment for users seeking long-term value. Connectivity options are comprehensive, including 5G, Wi-Fi 7, Bluetooth 6.0, NFC, and dual SIM support. These features ensure high-speed data transfer, seamless wireless connections, and broad network compatibility. The robust ecosystem integration with other Samsung devices further enhances its appeal for existing Samsung users.

Strengths

  • Equipped with the Qualcomm Snapdragon 8 Elite Gen 5, offering industry-leading processing power for all tasks.
  • Features dual 120 Hz Dynamic AMOLED 2X displays with up to 3000 nits brightness, providing stunning visuals and excellent outdoor visibility.
  • Offers an impressive seven years of software updates, ensuring long-term security and access to the latest features.
  • Improved 4800 mAh battery capacity delivers enhanced endurance, lasting longer even with intensive use.
  • Boasts IPX8 water resistance, an Armor Aluminum frame, and Gorilla Glass, providing robust durability against elements and drops.
  • The new form factor is thinner and lighter than previous Z Fold models, improving portability and handling comfort.

Weaknesses

  • The premium price point makes it one of the most expensive smartphones on the market, limiting accessibility for many buyers.
  • Lacks a dedicated telephoto camera, which might be a significant drawback for users prioritizing optical zoom capabilities.
  • Despite improvements, the foldable screen remains more delicate than traditional displays, requiring careful handling.
  • The device is still relatively thick and heavy when folded compared to conventional slab smartphones, impacting pocketability.
  • The 45W wired charging speed, while good, is not as fast as some competitors that offer 80W or 100W charging.
  • The 5.5-inch cover screen, while improved, is still smaller than the main display, potentially leading to a slightly cramped experience for some tasks when folded.

Things that change the decision

High Price Tag

The Samsung Galaxy Z Fold8 comes with a premium price, which can be a significant barrier for many potential buyers. This high cost places it firmly in the luxury smartphone segment, requiring a substantial investment.

Weight and Thickness
