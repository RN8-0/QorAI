Detailed analysis

The Roborock saros 10R is a high-performance robot vacuum and mop combination, designed to offer comprehensive floor cleaning. It features an impressive 19000 Pa suction power, which is highly effective for removing dirt and debris from various surfaces, including carpets and hard floors. The dual-rotating mop system, coupled with 80°C hot water washing, ensures superior stain removal and hygiene. Its advanced navigation system, powered by StarSight Otonom Sistem 2.0 and 3D ToF sensors, allows for precise mapping and intelligent obstacle avoidance. This enables the robot to navigate complex home environments, recognizing and bypassing up to 108 different obstacle types, including small items like cables. The fast mapping capability quickly generates accurate floor plans. Built with durable materials, the Saros 10R is designed for longevity and consistent performance. The multi-functional station 4.0 significantly enhances user convenience by automating mop washing, drying with hot air, dust emptying, and water refilling. This reduces the need for frequent manual maintenance, providing a more hands-off cleaning experience. The robot's ultra-slim profile, measuring just 7.98 cm in height, is a significant ergonomic advantage. This design allows it to easily access and clean under low-lying furniture, reaching areas that thicker robot vacuums often miss. This ensures a more thorough and complete cleaning of the entire home. While the Saros 10R offers extensive features, potential buyers should be aware of its relatively small dustbin and water tank capacities. These may require more frequent emptying and refilling, particularly in larger homes or those with pets. Additionally, some users have reported occasional issues with cable management, suggesting the need to clear cables before cleaning cycles. The device supports various smart home ecosystems through Alexa, Google Home, and Siri, offering convenient voice control. It also includes a built-in assistant activated by a custom command. However, it only supports 2.4 GHz Wi-Fi, which might be a minor consideration for homes with predominantly 5 GHz networks. Overall, the Roborock saros 10R is an excellent choice for users seeking a powerful, intelligent, and largely autonomous cleaning solution. Its combination of strong suction, effective mopping, and smart navigation makes it a top-tier option in the robot vacuum category, particularly for those with diverse flooring types and pets.

Strengths

  • Exceptional 19000 Pa suction power ensures deep cleaning on all floor types, effectively removing embedded dirt and pet hair.
  • Dual-rotating mops with 80°C hot water washing provide superior stain removal and hygienic floor cleaning.
  • Advanced StarSight Otonom Sistem 2.0 and 3D ToF sensors enable precise navigation, mapping, and obstacle avoidance for efficient cleaning paths.
  • Ultra-slim 7.98 cm design allows the robot to clean under low furniture, reaching areas inaccessible to bulkier models.
  • Multi-functional station 4.0 automates mop washing, drying, dust emptying, and water refilling, minimizing manual maintenance.
  • Comprehensive smart features include voice assistant support (Alexa, Google Home, Siri) and pet-friendly modes like tangle-free brushes and pet location detection.

Weaknesses

  • The dustbin capacity of 270 ml and water tank capacity of 69 ml are relatively small, requiring frequent emptying and refilling.
  • Only supports 2.4 GHz Wi-Fi, which may be a minor limitation for users with primarily 5 GHz home networks.
  • The robot may struggle with cable management, occasionally getting tangled in loose wires, necessitating pre-cleaning tidying.
  • The cleaning sequence is not fully customizable for room-by-room sweep-and-mop, instead performing all sweeping then all mopping.
  • The station's dust emptying process can be noisy, though it is a short duration event.

Things that change the decision

Cable Management Difficulties

The robot can occasionally struggle with cable detection, leading to entanglement during cleaning. Users are advised to clear cables before operation to prevent interruptions and potential damage.

Room-by-Room Cleaning Order Limitation

One user noted that the device first sweeps the entire house before starting to mop, rather than sweeping and mopping room by room. This might not suit users who prefer immediate, localized wet cleaning.

What owners say

The advanced navigation and obstacle recognition systems also receive high praise, with users appreciating the robot's intelligence in maneuvering around complex environments. The automatic station features, including self-emptying, mop washing with hot water, and hot air drying, are frequently highlighted as major conveniences. Additionally, a small number of users have reported less than ideal experiences with customer service, including issues with repairs or support responsiveness. Overall, the community views the Roborock saros 10R as a highly capable and feature-rich robot vacuum and mop. The positive feedback on its core cleaning performance and smart automation outweighs the criticisms regarding tank capacity and occasional cable issues, positioning it as a strong choice for those prioritizing powerful and intelligent floor care.

Frequently asked questions

What are the suction power and mopping performance of the Roborock Saros 10R?

The Saros 10R boasts an impressive 19000 Pa suction power, effortlessly cleaning deep dirt and pet hair from carpets and hard floors. Its dual rotary mop system and 80°C hot water automatic mop washing feature are highly effective against stubborn stains.

How are the navigation and obstacle recognition capabilities?

Thanks to the StarSight Autonomous System 2.0 and 3D ToF sensors, the device recognizes 108 different types of obstacles, minimizing entanglement during cleaning. This ensures an efficient and uninterrupted cleaning experience even in complex home environments.

What automatic station features does it offer?

The multi-functional station 4.0 handles numerous maintenance tasks, including automatic dust emptying, mop washing, mop drying, and water refilling. This minimizes user intervention, making the cleaning routine more effortless.

The 6400 mAh battery capacity provides up to 180 minutes of runtime in standard mode, sufficient for cleaning most homes on a single charge. Additionally, the 2.5-hour fast charging feature ensures the device is quickly ready for use again.

Are there any drawbacks to the Roborock Saros 10R?

The product's small dustbin (270 ml) and water tank (69 ml) capacities may require frequent emptying/refilling, especially in large homes or for pet owners. Also, its support for only 2.4 GHz Wi-Fi can be a limitation.
