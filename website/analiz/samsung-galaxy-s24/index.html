Detailed analysis

The Samsung Galaxy S24 stands out as a premium Android smartphone, offering a compelling blend of compact design, powerful hardware, and advanced features. It is equipped with either the Samsung Exynos 2400 or Qualcomm Snapdragon 8 Gen 3 chipset, depending on the region, ensuring top-tier performance for all tasks. Performance is a significant strength, with its 10-core CPU and 8 GB of RAM delivering smooth multitasking and efficient handling of demanding applications and games. While some reports mention potential warming during extended gaming sessions, this is generally within acceptable limits for high-performance devices. The camera system is robust, featuring a 50 MP main sensor, 12 MP ultra-wide, and 10 MP telephoto lens. It supports 8K video recording and includes AI-powered enhancements like Portrait Mode and ProVisual Engine, which improve image quality and offer creative shooting options. However, the absence of autofocus on the ultra-wide camera is a notable limitation. The 6.2-inch Dynamic LTPO AMOLED 2X display is a highlight, boasting FHD+ resolution, an adaptive 120 Hz refresh rate, and an impressive 2600 nits peak brightness. This combination ensures vibrant colors, smooth scrolling, and excellent visibility even in direct sunlight, all protected by Corning Gorilla Glass Victus 2. Battery life from the 4000 mAh unit is generally adequate for a full day of moderate use, supported by 25W fast charging and wireless charging. However, heavy users might find it necessary to recharge before the day ends, and the lack of a charging adapter in the box is an inconvenience that adds to the initial cost. Samsung's commitment to seven years of operating system and security updates is a major advantage, ensuring the Galaxy S24 remains secure and feature-rich for an extended period. This long-term software support significantly enhances the device's longevity and value proposition. Built with a premium glass back and an aluminum frame, the S24 exudes a high-quality feel and offers IPX8 water resistance for added durability. Its compact form factor is a key selling point for users seeking a powerful phone that is easy to handle and carry. Connectivity options are comprehensive, including 5G support, Bluetooth 5.3, Wi-Fi 6E, and a versatile USB Type-C port. Dual SIM capability further adds to its utility. However, the absence of a MicroSD card slot means storage cannot be expanded beyond the internal capacity.

Weaknesses

  • No MicroSD card slot means storage cannot be expanded beyond the internal capacity.

Things that change the decision

No MicroSD Card Support

The Samsung Galaxy S24 does not offer expandable storage via a MicroSD card slot, meaning users are limited to the internal storage option chosen at purchase. This can be a drawback for those who require extensive local storage for media or files.

Galaxy AI Features Free for Limited Duration

The advanced Galaxy AI features on the S24 are offered free of charge only until the end of 2025. After this period, users may need to pay for continued access, which could be a hidden long-term cost.

What owners say

Satisfaction: 82/100

Community sentiment for the Samsung Galaxy S24 is largely positive, with a significant majority of users expressing satisfaction with their purchase. The compact size and premium build quality are frequently highlighted as key advantages, appealing to those who prefer a more ergonomic device without compromising on flagship features. The stunning Dynamic AMOLED display, with its high brightness and adaptive 120Hz refresh rate, also receives widespread acclaim for enhancing the visual experience. Performance is another area where the S24 excels in user feedback. Owners consistently report a fluid and responsive experience, whether navigating the UI, running multiple applications, or engaging in demanding mobile gaming. The powerful chipset ensures that the phone handles intensive tasks with ease, contributing to a high level of user satisfaction. However, there are recurring concerns, particularly regarding battery life. While Samsung suggests up to two days of use, many users find that under typical or heavy usage, the device often requires recharging within a single day. This discrepancy between expectation and actual experience is a common theme in negative feedback, especially among users who game frequently or stream a lot of content. Another point of contention is the absence of a charging adapter in the box. This decision, while common among modern flagships, continues to be a source of frustration for consumers who feel it represents an additional, unexpected cost. The lack of an autofocus feature on the ultra-wide camera is also mentioned by some reviewers as a missed opportunity for a phone in this price bracket. Despite these criticisms, the overall perception remains strong, largely due to Samsung's commitment to long-term software support. The promise of seven years of OS and security updates is a significant factor contributing to positive sentiment, as it assures users of the device's longevity and continued relevance. This commitment helps to mitigate some of the minor hardware-related complaints by offering substantial future-proofing.

Who is it for?

Buy it if: This phone is ideal for users prioritizing a compact, premium smartphone experience with flagship performance and a commitment to long-term software support. Its excellent display and camera system make it suitable for multimedia consumption and photography enthusiasts.

Skip it if: It is not ideal for users who require extensive battery life for continuous heavy usage or those who depend on expandable storage via MicroSD cards. Buyers on a strict budget might also find the initial cost, plus the need for a separate charger, a deterrent.

Verdict

Frequently asked questions

What is the battery life of the Samsung Galaxy S24?

With a 4000 mAh battery capacity, the Samsung Galaxy S24's battery life might fall short of expectations during heavy use. Average screen-on time is reported to be around 5-7 hours.

Is the Galaxy S24 camera good?

The 50 MP main camera, with 8K video recording and AI-powered features, captures high-quality photos and videos. However, the lack of autofocus on the ultra-wide-angle camera is a limitation.

Is the Samsung Galaxy S24 suitable for gaming?

The Exynos 2400 or Snapdragon 8 Gen 3 chipset and 8 GB RAM provide top-tier performance, sufficient for daily tasks and demanding games. It may show a tendency to heat up during intense gaming sessions.

How long does Galaxy S24 software support last?

Samsung commits to seven years of operating system and security updates for the Galaxy S24, ensuring the device remains current and secure for a long period.

Does the Samsung Galaxy S24 come with a charger?

No, the Samsung Galaxy S24 does not include a charging adapter in the box. This means users will incur an additional cost to purchase a separate adapter.

Is the Samsung Galaxy S24 water resistant?

Yes, the Samsung Galaxy S24 has an IPX8 water resistance rating. This protects the device against water immersion and provides durability against everyday accidents.
