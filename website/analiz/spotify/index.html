Frequently asked questions

What are the main benefits of Spotify Premium?

Spotify Premium offers ad-free listening, unlimited skips, and offline download features. It is indispensable for listening to music while traveling or in places without an internet connection.

Is Spotify good for discovering new music?

Yes, Spotify's personalized recommendation algorithms, such as 'Discover Weekly' and 'Daily Mixes,' make it effortless to explore new artists and genres. Its vast music library also continuously provides new content.

What is the sound quality like on Spotify?

Lossless audio support (24-bit/44.1 kHz FLAC) is available with a Premium subscription, which is a significant plus for users who prioritize sound quality. The free version offers lower sound quality.

Are there issues with Spotify subscription cancellation or payments?

Some users report issues such as unauthorized payment deductions or the subscription cancellation button not working. Difficulties in reaching customer support can also occur, making careful subscription management important.

On which devices can Spotify be used?

Spotify works seamlessly on many different devices, including phones, computers, smartwatches, and televisions. With Spotify Connect, you can start music on one device and continue it on another.
