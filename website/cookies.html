🍪 Cookie Policy

Cookies & Storage

We keep tracking to a minimum. This page explains the cookies and local storage qorai.net uses, what each is for, and how you can control them.

🔒 Strictly necessary

These are required for the site to function and cannot be switched off:

  • Theme preference — remembers your dark/light choice (local storage).
  • Language — remembers your selected language (local storage).
  • Session — keeps you signed in after you log in (local storage token).

📊 Analytics

We use privacy-respecting, aggregated analytics to understand which pages and features are useful. This data is anonymized and never used to build advertising profiles or sold to third parties.

🤝 Advertising & affiliate attribution

qorai.net displays ads via Google AdSense and earns commissions through affiliate links. These partners may set cookies to measure ad performance and to attribute a purchase to a click from our site.

Affiliate attribution only records that a click came from Qor AI so a retailer can credit a resulting sale — it does not change the price you pay. See our affiliate disclosure for details.

⚙️ Managing cookies

You can clear or block cookies and local storage at any time in your browser settings. Blocking strictly-necessary storage may sign you out or reset your theme/language. For more on how we handle data, read our Privacy Policy.
