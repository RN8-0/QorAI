🔍 How it works

We aggregate technical specifications, prices and real community opinions for every product, then score and rank them with AI so the trade-offs are obvious at a glance.

  • AI-scored specs — each product gets a normalized technical score within its category.
  • Community Voice — we surface real opinions from Reddit, forums, Trustpilot and YouTube.
  • Personal match — a short quiz tailors a compatibility score to your needs and budget.
  • Side-by-side compare — line up 2–4 products and see the winner instantly.

⚖️ Editorial independence & affiliate disclosure

Our scores and rankings are generated from objective specifications and aggregated reviews — they are never influenced by commercial relationships.

Qor AI is reader-supported. Some links to retailers are affiliate links: if you buy through them we may earn a commission at no extra cost to you. This never changes which products we rank highest or how we score them. The price shown for a retailer always comes from that same retailer's offer.
