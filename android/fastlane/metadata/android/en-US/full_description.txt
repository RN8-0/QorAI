If you are tired of spending hours researching before you buy, Qor AI is built for you.

It puts phones, laptops, graphics cards, headphones, TVs, smartwatches, tablets and PC parts side by side, then shows the tech score, the current price and the specs that actually matter — all on one screen. No more twenty open tabs.

▸ PASTE A LINK, GET AN ANALYSIS
Paste a product link from any store. The AI reviews that product, tells you whether the price is fair, and gives you a clear verdict: buy, think about it, or skip.

▸ UP TO 4 PRODUCTS SIDE BY SIDE
Compare four products from the same category at once. See which specs genuinely matter and which ones are just marketing.

▸ TECH SCORE
One number per product. Dozens of specs — from charging speed to refresh rate — condensed into a single score so comparing takes seconds.

▸ COMPARE SUBSCRIPTIONS TOO
Streaming, VPN, hosting and more. Put digital subscriptions side by side on price and what you actually get.

▸ BUILD A PC
Assemble a PC from scratch with compatibility checks. Does the motherboard match the CPU, is the power supply enough — the app tells you.

▸ SCAN WITH YOUR CAMERA
Point your camera at a product box in a store to identify it and pull up its specs.

▸ AI CHAT
Ask "which of these two is better for gaming?" and get an answer from an assistant that knows the product. Keep asking follow-ups.

▸ PERSONALIZED PICKS
A short quiz learns your budget and priorities, and recommendations adapt to them.
